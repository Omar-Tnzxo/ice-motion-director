# Final-film QC protocol (original film: checked against the storyboard and the style guide, not against an original film)

> Replace <project root> with the absolute path of the working directory; vN is the version number; Cn is the chapter number.

Input: the final film frame by frame `<project root>/fin_frames/f_%04d.jpg` (1-based), the thumbnail overview `<project root>/renders/sheet_vN.html`, `<project root>/storyboard.md` (frame ranges / beats / on-screen intent), `<project root>/script/timeline.md` (subtitle block frame numbers), the skill's `reference/style-guide.md` and `reference/motion-vocabulary.md`, `<project root>/research/research.md`, and each group's `<project root>/src/shots/Gn/BUILD_NOTES.md` (known downgrades, do not report them again).
Output: `<project root>/qc/qc_vN_Cn.md`, **append as you check**. Each entry: `| Severity high/medium/low | Shot | Frame | Symptom | Criterion | Suggested fix |`.

> English films (`lang: 'en'` in `config.ts`) add two more: a subtitle wrapped onto two lines (meaning the block exceeds the 48-character budget and has been pushed into the content area) → medium; a chapter name on the progress bar or a chapter card title automatically shrunk until it looks blurry → low, go back and shorten the chapter name.

## What to check (in priority order)
1. **Readability / occlusion**: any text covered by or touching (<10px) the subtitle band (y637-690), the progress bar (y687-720), the HUD (y28-100), or the process track (chapters with a track, y112-160, see the storyboard's "Persistent layers"); text overflowing the canvas; font size <22px; white text on white / light blocks; grey text unreadable on the fog backdrop.
2. **Beats**: appearance frame of key elements vs the corresponding subtitle block start frame (timeline.md): late by >3 frames or early by >6 frames → medium; a whole sentence with no corresponding on-screen change → high. Method: look at four frames, subtitle block start frame −6, 0, +3, +8.
3. **Facts / spelling**: check every English word and number on screen against the research document; typos / mixed traditional and simplified characters / inconsistent terminology (the same concept written differently by two groups) → medium.
4. **Style consistency**: colors outside the palette (not purple / orange-red / grey / white / green check), wrong fonts (Chinese not Noto, English technical words not Exo 2 italic), stroke width (2-3px), corner-radius style, glitch entrance missing or excessive (>12 frames of flicker), an opaque black background covering the backdrop (large areas of pure black, no star points / dot field).
5. **Continuity**: boundary frames between adjacent shots (from/to of each storyboard row): whether elements suddenly disappear / jump / are drawn twice across the two frames; at group boundaries (G1|G2 …) especially check whether the HUD / process track overlaps content; at **chapter boundaries** (3 frames before and after the chapter card + the first 30 frames of the chapter's first shot): whether the chapter's first shot is a completely fresh picture, and whether the hand-off elements listed in the storyboard's "Chapter boundary list" were done → medium; also check whether the chapter's opening narration refers back to the previous chapter (starting from zero or only announcing the topic → low, report to the main session; for the cost of changing the wording see item 11); whether content is left over before the chapter card; fragment shots <90 frames that still have a full entrance + exit → low (they should be merged into an adjacent shot as a beat).
6. **Animation quality**: sample 3 places per shot with 3 consecutive frames each (entrance midpoint / middle / exit midpoint) and check for jitter, reversed direction, stiff easing (linear large displacements), elements "popping in" from off-screen (already at their end position on the first frame), the kf first-value trap (the shot starts frozen in a mid-state).
7. **Performance artifacts**: grey haze from excessive blur / glow, gradient banding, jagged text edges.
8. **Composition and light** (`composition-and-light.md` §6): first run `python3 <project root>/scripts/frame_metrics.py --frames <project root>/fin_frames --storyboard <project root>/storyboard.md --out <project root>/qc/frame_metrics_vN.md`, merge this chapter's shot flags into the report one by one, and confirm by looking at the frames: content-area subject scale <110px with no large-area light activity for >45 frames → medium (<80px → high; the sweep / light-line phases do not count as an empty stage); median subject scale <170 → low; no soft-glow spot in the hero area of a set-piece shot → medium; ≥8 purple fragments in one frame → low; ≥10 small shapes scattered in the background → medium (point clouds / square-dot arrays trigger this falsely; look at the frame to confirm whether it is a regular matrix or random debris). **Three-pass purple light sweeps / stage light lines / ghost outlines in shots outside the light-sweep whitelist → medium** (≤2 in the whole film, only for the core concept's entrance and an optional callback at the end).
9. **Continuous action and settle hold** (composition-and-light.md §7): first run `python3 <project root>/scripts/motion_check.py --frames <project root>/fin_frames`. For shots whose longest stillness is >3 s, look at the frames: full-resolution changed pixels <800 = truly still → medium (give the action matching the verb, **do not suggest adding floating / breathing**); 800-2500 = small-area action → low (suggest a larger amplitude). Last-beat stable period <30 frames (from the last-beat elements settling to the exit start) → medium: suggest delaying the exit / pulling the last-beat elements forward / merging into an adjacent shot, not adding action. A camera move not finished within the 30 frames before the exit → medium. The stillness ratio is reference only and is not a defect on its own.
10. **Camera moves**: check each item of the storyboard's "Camera move list" at the first / middle / last frame: whether the push-in centers on the hero, whether it is a 30-45 frame easeInOut (linear or <15 frames → medium), whether there is a glitch or staggered entrance during the move (→ medium), whether the HUD / process track / subtitles get carried along (→ high); fewer than 3 camera moves in a chapter → low.

11. **Navigation labels**: the chapter names on the progress bar (one phrase per chapter) and the HUD section names: looking only at these words, can you tell what that part is about? Metaphors, evaluative sentences or suspense words (bad examples `"feels okay"` / `"two rulers"`) → low, give a suggested rename directly. **Renaming is cheap**: chapter names live only in the `# CHAPTER n name` lines of `narration.txt`, HUD words only in `hud[].text` of `config.ts`; neither affects frame numbers or the voice-over.

## How to check
- First read through the whole film once via `sheet_vN.html` (one image every 60 frames) and list suspicious shots; then for each shot look at single frames at each "beat frame" per the storyboard; finally look at consecutive frames per §5/§6.
- Look at images with Read; when you need to quantify (bbox / color / brightness), use Python PIL (sum brightness as int32; int16 overflows). Do not modify any source code.
- Flicker compliance (final check): for each shot's element entrance frames, build a brightness curve over f0…f0+12; 0/0.5/1 jumps = flicker; the number of hits must equal the number of whitelist entries.
- Give at least one conclusion line per shot (write "OK" too), to guarantee coverage.

## Severity
- High: unreadable / covered text, a whole sentence with no picture, factual errors, missing components (in the storyboard but not on screen), a black background covering the backdrop, empty stage (subject <80px for >45 frames), a camera move carrying the HUD / subtitles along.
- Medium: beat deviations, style inconsistency, continuity jumps, spelling, subject <110px for >45 frames, set-piece hero without light, background debris, stiff camera moves or camera moves overlapping a glitch, longest stillness >3 s, last-beat stable period <30 frames (cut right on settling), camera move intruding into the last beat.
- Low: fine adjustments (spacing / alignment / brightness), optional optimizations.
