# Slop scan

The slop scan looks for the defaults an AI reaches for instead of deciding. Run it on the plan (storyboard and copy), on the composition source, and on the contact sheet of the render. A film containing a never-ship item is not finished: delete the element or show its plan or exemption row. Every ban here is a default for the film types it names (lesson L14): a device it bans is used only when the user's own words, a measured reference value, the chosen style's craft profile or a content trigger recorded in the craft plan asks for it, in the order and with the row format of the `ice-direction` skill, `references/treatment.md` section "Craft plan" (section 4 here, "Measured and declared packages"). "Justified" with no recorded source still fails, and truth, rights, safety, accessibility, legibility, Arabic correctness and the speaker staying lit are never lifted. Sources: motionmaxxing `slop.md` and `judgment.md` (Apache, wording kept where quoted), product-launch-motion, motion-video-kit, Barty-Bart, charlie947, bang-motion, text-to-lottie (MIT), hyperframes docs and skills (Apache), huashu-art-motion (MIT), herk-edit-kit (MIT), explainroo, video-ad-editor, founder skills; IDEAS_ONLY sources (imMamdouhaboammar, vibe-motion, PolyForm explainer sources) rewritten in our words.

## 1. The 12 never-ship items

Each item is never shipped as a reach or a decoration. Invented stats and proof (item 5), self-certification (item 11) and a film you did not look at (item 12) are truth and process rules and are never lifted; any other device an item names passes only with its plan or exemption row (section 4, "Measured and declared packages"). The news reel scope lines below are the measured broadcast case of that rule.

1. Page chrome or HUD: a brand or film name in a corner, tracked-caps labels, counters ("02 / SPEAK"), timecodes, BPM, FPS or resolution text, progress bars, corner brackets, scene labels over a shot. A recurring identity mark chosen at 01 (illustration only: a channel bug, a series title, a progress bar) passes when the plan's `identity` row names it and the element carries `data-identity-mark` (`pre-render-lint.md`). Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, the logo bug, the programme name, the reel progress bar, the location and date tag and the counter passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").
2. Deck layouts: a held left-aligned headline block, a kicker above a headline, a title then content, header, body and footer, "headline left, device right".
3. Generic web-component UI: a rounded card with a status dot and "label . $value", a "Good morning" card, toolbar emoji, zinc greys, skeleton bars; invented names or numbers where the capture has real ones. Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, the glass key-phrase card with a dot growing per bullet passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").
4. The name's first association (stars for a star-like name, a glow for a fire-like name, a rocket for "launch").
5. Invented stats and proof, stat walls, a stat slide (centred big number over a small label), on-screen disclaimers the rules do not require. Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, a spoken, true number counted on the speaker's beat (the counter with colour change) passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").

Never slop: a label or disclaimer the rules require ("Example data", "Illustration", "Concept screen", the unofficial-concept line, a required disclaimer from `ice/research/COMPLIANCE.md`), set as the ice-assets skill's `truth-sheet.md` section 9 ("How a required on-screen label is set") or section 12 says. It is exempt from items 1, 2 and 5, from N1, N2 and N21, and from text-density counts; one set in another form (a corner badge, tracked caps, a pill) is still item 1.
6. Slogan copy: triplets of one-word sentences, "Not just X. It's Y.", "Unlock", "Meet X", "the future of".
7. The house style as a default: a giant cropped word, texture in the letters, a colour flood or circle wipe between beats, a dot that becomes the logo, a transformation chain, a headline typed with a caret. Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, scanline and glow texture on titles, the ghost programme title and the typed question card (a mask reveal over the joined line) passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").
8. Flat swatch grounds per scene, or a small card on a void (a 0.3 W object for a 1 s read in empty ground).
9. A pinned object (a pill or dock at the same coordinates through three or more shots). Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, a fixed identity mark (logo bug, programme name, progress bar) held in its measured slot passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").
10. A web-footer ending: logo, tagline, button and URL, or an end card held past the type's limit (1.4 s for launch-style films; see `motion-density.md` section 4). Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, the branded end card held up to the `news reel` row of `motion-density.md` section 6 passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").
11. Self-certification: no delivery report, gates claimed without script numbers, "justified" fails.
12. A film you did not look at.

(motionmaxxing; item 10 scoped by film type in this repo.)

## 3. Default reaches and the second-order house style (run on the plan)

Twelve reaches, each with its plan question:
1. Making a page instead of staging a picture: would this frame work as a slide or a website hero?
2. Showing everything with equal weight: what is the one true thing; which features did I leave out on purpose?
3. States instead of events: does the plan say what moves and what it causes, or only what is shown?
4. Premium pastiche (near-black, radial glow, gold spark, serif-italic word, tracked small caps; six of ten AI films sat on near-black, under a third of designer films did and almost half were bright). Scope: in a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, style family `family-broadcast-news.md`), or when the reference measures it, the radial glow disc with its trigger passes with its reason row (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)").
5. Production metadata as decoration.
6. Filling holes with invented proof: did I open the captured screens and media first?
7. The first association of the name ("launch" gets a rocket, security gets a shield).
8. Effects standing in for quality: a new technique every beat to show range.
9. A script with illustrations: words first, a picture under each sentence (keep the writer: the copy is often the best part).
10. Sound as wallpaper: a fixed loop at a flat level, an onset every half second, no silence, no build; or silence by default.
11. The ending as a web footer: did the logo arrive by cause, is the ending smaller than the climax, is it short?
12. Minimalism as a hiding place: is more than a third of the film nearly still; where is the frame someone would screenshot?

Second-order house style (the "designed" default, measured in a 21-direction study: giant cropped word 20 of 21, material inside the letters 17, flood or circle wipe 19, dot-to-logo 11, a lockup built from the film's motif 21; eight brands read as one designer's reel in eight colourways): a giant word cropped off the frame edge in every film; a repeated-word marquee band; texture or video filling the letters; a colour flood or circle wipe between every beat; a dot that becomes the logo's dot; a lockup built from the motif; a text strip between rows of texture; everything moving all the time with holds forbidden; every film a chain of "X becomes Y becomes the logo". As proof of effort it is the new slop.

Self-check: would this pass the competitor swap (`identity-tests.md`), or is it the same film I would make for another brand? Which choices did I decide, and which did I reach for? Removing tells without adding substance gives clean, hollow films that lose to a plain film built on the brand's own material.

### Measured and declared packages

Scope: every film type (lesson L14, 0.2.5). A measured package is the set of devices a reference shows (its `REASONS.md` rows, its `style-recipe.json`, its finishing, depth and sound measurements); a declared package is the set the chosen style's craft profile lists, or that the user's own words or a content trigger in the craft plan call for. Inside either, a device this file bans is used, and only when all three hold (the same three as the broadcast case below):

1. **It has its row.** A craft plan row with its trigger, reason and source, or an exemption row whose `lifts` names the hit (`slop:<item number or N id>`, or the gate's own id), with the shots and the amount; format and precedence: the `ice-direction` skill, `references/treatment.md` section "Craft plan". The gate prints it under "exempt" with the row id and its source.
2. **It follows its numbers**: the measured value (within its tolerance) or the value the style or the user states, and the identity choice of stage 01 for any outside skin.
3. **It keeps the never rules and is well made**: the never-lifted rules of the `ice-direction` skill, `references/treatment.md` section 11.7 stay absolute, and so does no text over a face; and the execution check of its home file (a card field has parallax, occlusion and shadows; a glow sits at its measured or stated radius; a typed Arabic line is a mask reveal over the shaped line).

Decoration without a source still fails, in every film type: an element with no row, on a shot its row does not name, above its amount, or carried over when its reason does not hold in this film. A package is never a default look for an unrelated request (anti-sameness, AGENTS.md rule 2). The broadcast package below is the measured case this rule grew from; the finishing effects row of section 5 is another case.

### Broadcast package (news reel)

Scope (owner decision, 2026-10-29; one case of "Measured and declared packages" above): a news reel (edit type `news-reel`, the `ice-edit` skill, `references/edit-types/news-reel.md`; style family `family-broadcast-news.md`, the `ice-styles` skill, section 1), or any film whose reference measures the element. Inside that scope each element below replaces the never-ship item, pattern or ban it names, and only when all three hold:

1. **It has its reason row**: a row in the reference's `REASONS.md` (the `ice-remake` skill, `references/notes-files.md` section 6) or, with no reference, a storyboard row whose Trigger quotes the spoken words and whose Reason names the subject role and the job of its colour (`storyboard-lint.md` section 3b). The row's trigger exists in this film's script, on the same kind of subject.
2. **It follows its numbers** in its canonical home (below), and the package skin (hex values, fonts, positions) only when the stage 01 `identity` slot is "as they are" or "a mix"; otherwise the same role logic carries the user's own palette, fonts and marks (`family-broadcast-news.md` section 2; the `ice-remake` skill, `references/rights-and-modes.md` section 1).
3. **It keeps the genre's never rules**: the speaker stays lit (the `ice-edit` skill, `references/layers-and-isolation.md`, "The speaker stays lit"), no text over a face, spoken words are never paraphrased on screen (end cards excepted), and logos and the progress bar are never moved by the camera or hit by a flash.

Decoration without a reason still fails inside a news reel: an element with no reason row, on a shot the row does not name, above its measured amount or count, or carried over when its reason does not hold in this film is a never-ship hit, whatever the genre. A package is never a default look for an unrelated request (anti-sameness, AGENTS.md rule 2). In the composition, mark each element `data-broadcast="<element>"` so the page-chrome lint lists it under its exemptions instead of failing it (`pre-render-lint.md` section 4).

## 5. When a "tell" is right

| Tell | Works when |
|---|---|
| Typewriter with caret | The product is something you type into, or the caret becomes something (a divider, a letter of the logo); never as a reveal style on every line or a headline by reflex. Typing as the medium or the meaning (a terminal, chat or typewriter style, a typed card a reference measures) passes with its plan row (section 4, typewriter row); in a news reel or a measured reference: the typed question card (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)") |
| Centred still end card | It follows sustained motion and echoes the opening; the logo arrived by cause; short |
| Soft gradient or glow | It is the brand's own material, a named object with a job, one light source that changes with the mood, or the chosen style's medium is light (section 4, glow row). Glow limits: the `ice-motion` skill, `references/composition-and-staging.md` section 6 |
| Serif in a sans film | The brand's own face, or a deliberate second voice used rarely and large |
| Dissolve or blur | It copies a real gesture (focus pull, scroll, whip) with a direction, or the dissolve is the meaning; calm brands may fade; the type's transition policy allows it or the reference measures it (N24); a news reel cold open dissolves its image beats when the package or the reference measures it (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)") |
| Hard colour flips | The colour change is the story beat and an object carries across the cut |
| Slide grammar | Paced to a voice at speech rate, type never changes position, a second visual voice adds wit; never as a page |
| Mostly empty frame | Conditions: the `ice-motion` skill, `references/composition-and-staging.md` section 2 (conflict note) |
| A number counting up | The number is the real offer and its size is the point, tied to a cause |
| Template moves | Deliberate quotation (satire: bland visuals against loaded copy) |
| Low frame rate, no blur | Used consistently as a chosen texture |
| Finishing effects (motion blur, blur for depth, blur on entrances and exits, grain, specks, halftone, flicker, vignette, glow, grade) | A reference measurement (the `ice-remake` skill, `references/measuring.md`, "Finishing layer") or a D-entry in `ice/DIRECTION.md` names the shots, the amount and the reason, and the film applies it there at that amount (how: the `ice-motion` skill, `references/motion-blur-and-clock.md`, "Finishing layer"). Scope: every film type (owner decision, 2026-10-29). The D-entry is the craft plan's `finish` row, written for every film at 05 (the `ice-direction` skill, `references/treatment.md` section "Craft plan"); this row is one case of section 4, "Measured and declared packages". It still fails as decoration with no source, on shots the source does not name, or above the measured amount; the rows above (N11, N20, the glow row of section 4) apply in full to an effect with no source |
| Monospace headline | It does a job (reads like a bill or a log) and gives way to the brand font when the product arrives |
| Fade to black, a calm ending | The brand is calm and the film breathes throughout (22 of 51 gold films end calmly) |
| Giant cropped word | The idea wants that word huge, it arrives once with a cause, and scale is not spent again |
| Dot-to-logo, motif lockup, chain | It is this brand's idea (its mark really is that shape) and each link is true |
| 3D, depth or tilted UI | The idea is physical or spatial, a cropped held-pose device, or a card field: cards posed inside a field of planes with measured parallax, occlusion, contact shadows and a camera moving through or around them, each card with its trigger and reason row (N16; the `ice-motion` skill, `references/depth-and-3d.md` sections 1 and 7). Never a lone tilted card as "depth" (one flat card skewed by perspective with nothing else at depth) |
| Page chrome | Never as decoration. Passes with its row: a recurring identity mark chosen at 01 (the plan's `identity` row and `data-identity-mark`, item 1); a HUD, OSD or stories-style mark that the chosen style's craft profile names or a reference measures, in its slot (section 4, "Measured and declared packages"); a programme's real marks in a news reel or a measured reference (section 4, "Broadcast package (news reel)") |

Nothing here is a rule you pass: with a better reason you have a better film, but say the reason in one sentence about this brand (motionmaxxing) and record it as a plan or exemption row. The skill's own defaults are options, not the house style: arrival from oversize, the carrier object and the built-from-parts logo become slop when every film uses them.

## 6. Copy tells (run on the script and every on-screen line)

- "Not just X. It's Y." and "More than X, it's Y."; triplets of one-word sentences; "Unlock your potential", "Meet X", "Introducing the future of", "superpowers", "seamless", "effortless", "reimagined", "all-in-one", "Revolutionize"; "Learn more" or "Get started free" as the last line; a chain of puns on the brand name; invented numbers, users, testimonials; a line that could be pasted into any other launch film unchanged (motionmaxxing, founder skills).
- Slogans and punchlines, "not X, but Y" twists, colon reveals, chains of three short sentences, filler words ("just", "simply", "really", "actually"), telling the viewer obvious things ("Let's dive in", "Stay tuned"); dash characters between phrases (explainroo).
- Reveal-declaratives ("Nobody signed these"), fake-candid openers ("Here's the trick"), restating what the demo proved, editorializing qualifiers; anything that fails the deletion test (vox-explainer).
- Unverified superlatives (cheapest, best, number one, certified, fresh daily) unless the owner approved the exact words; a time range from the source never rounded into a promise (founder skills).
- Triple adjectives in one phrase; on-screen disclaimers that no law or platform requires.
- In Arabic dialect films, translated formal phrases that do not belong to the dialect; dialect rules are owned by the ice-arabic skill.
- Title Case benefit lists read as a template; sentence case with a full stop reads as a person (motionmaxxing). A full stop after every two-word headline reads stiff (bang-motion). Write like the brand speaks, then cut half; an official launch line verbatim beats a stock one.

## 7. How to report

Write `ice/review/slop-scan.md`: one line per hit with ID, timecode or plan row, evidence (frame path or source line), and the instead-action. A hit on sections 1-2 is a blocker: delete the element or show its plan or exemption row (the `ice-direction` skill, `references/treatment.md` section "Craft plan"; section 4, "Measured and declared packages"); an element a row covers is reported under "exempt" with the row id and its source, never argued in prose. A section 4 "question" needs a one-sentence reason in `ice/DIRECTION.md` or it becomes a blocker. Structural slop to also name: equal-emphasis frames, card soup, a scene sampler, layout repetition, a busy ending; typography slop: everything in the heaviest weight, transcript wallpaper, font-demo features, narrow headline walls, accidental crops; material slop: default fake premium, texture as dirt, inconsistent shadows, mixed asset worlds; review slop: vague notes, redesign disguised as polish, endless polishing (imMamdouhaboammar, rewritten). Final subtraction: remove any effect that does not improve meaning, hierarchy, continuity, material, emotion or brand; technical difficulty is not a reason to keep it.
