# Adaptation lenses: from the reference to the user's film

Read this when writing the Treatment. The remake keeps what made the reference work and rebuilds it around the user's subject, product, words, platform and look. Fuller original text: `vendor/hypit/transformations.md`.

## 1. Preserve the role, recreate the form

- Keep the reference's semantic timing relationships and bind them to the target's own words and actions. Seconds change; relationships stay. Keep a literal clock interval only when the interval itself is the design.
- Before replacing anything, list every place its role appears. A presenter may also be a voice, a B-roll subject, an image on a phone, an avatar inside a graphic. A product may be a prop, a screen, a demo, a claim and the CTA. Transform all of them, not just the most visible one.
- Keep an action's purpose, not a form that no longer fits the new subject. Confidence can be posture, gaze or framing; a product without a screen proves the same benefit with different evidence.
- When a system is removed (no presenter, no voiceover, no captions), hand its job to another system. Never leave a narrative hole.

## 2. Lenses

| Lens | Rethink |
|---|---|
| Subject or identity | Who carries the narrative role and everywhere that identity appears. Real people's faces and voices: `ice-assets/references/ai-media-policy.md` section 9; the reference's own footage of people is never placed in the film |
| Product or brand | Product truth, use, props, UI, logo, colors, proof, offer, CTA. Claims only from the user's facts (the ice-assets skill, truth sheet) |
| Script, language or CTA | Meaning, speech rhythm, on-screen words, demos, and the timing that follows the new words. Arabic or mixed: the ice-arabic skill |
| Platform, duration or aspect | Which story functions survive, compress, expand or need new composition. Re-layout per aspect, never crop. Platform specs: the ice-engines skill |
| Visual or tonal style | Art direction across picture, captions, type, graphics, effects and sound, without erasing the structure that works. A restyle changes edges, shadows, type, texture and motion character, not just color |
| Adding or removing a system | Redistribute its job |
| Several references | One function per reference (`mixing-references.md`) |

These are prompts for thought, not fields to fill. Follow the relationships the actual work has.

## 3. Product swaps

Rewrite the objection, demo, prop, coverage and CTA from the product's real benefit. The reference's moment of understanding (a skeptical question answered by a demonstration) survives; its content is rebuilt from what the user's product actually does. Unclear claims are settled with the user while creative work continues.

## 4. Jokes and personal references

Jokes about the original presenter's looks, life or persona are rethought for the new subject; they may become a better joke. Humor reaches the viewer through words, situation, reaction or delivery, never through an odd look alone.

## 5. Compare by function

Judge the remake by whether each transformed element does the same job at its corresponding word or action, not by pixel similarity. Where the Treatment deliberately changes the job, the Treatment is the authority. (Frame-locked mode adds pixel-level checks: `frame-locked-remake.md`.)
