# Shot blueprints and constructions

Time-coded shot templates and shot constructions. They are a soft menu: story truth comes first; a blueprint suggests a proven shape and never dictates which beats exist. Postures: reproduce, adapt (state what is kept and changed; never drop the signature move), or compose from scratch. Do not force a wrong blueprint. Take the mechanism, invent the picture: a film must differ from its nearest template on at least 4 of 6 axes (AGENTS.md rule 2). Film-type structures (hook, proof, CTA order, word budgets) are owned by the ice-film and ice-explainer workflows; this file owns the motion inside a shot.

## 1. Twenty-two blueprints (launch genre, reverse-engineered from 178 clips)

Roles map to storyboard frames: hook, problem, product intro, key feature, benefits, social proof, CTA, brand outro. Reveals are paced to the spoken line, never dumped at t = 0.

| Blueprint | Length | Signature move | Camera rule |
|---|---|---|---|

| Typewriter reveal | 3.6-7 s | A caret types or edits, then the brand pops | locked |

## 2. Fourteen shot constructions (narrated launch films)

| Shot | Length | Construction | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic type beats | 3.5-5 s | 3-5 lines, one emphasis verb each; stale lines drop to 0.30 opacity; rise 28 px over 0.3 s on the measured verb; the last line as three micro-hits; final 4 px settle and about 8 percent ground brighten; camera locked | the rhythm is even |
| Crowding problem | 3.5-4.5 s | 6-9 chips across 3 depth planes, index-derived scatter, about 0.14 s apart; close in on the conjunction; the crowd dims to about 0.32 | |
| Held thesis | 1.8-2.5 s | Deliberately the shortest frame; second half at about 0.80 opacity without accent; one wipe through the hinge word; last 0.4 s locked; near silence | |
| Prompt or composer | 2-3 s | One typewriter tween; the button fill swaps its label at about 12 percent fill; leave the last step running; no per-keystroke sound | typing invented text instead of the real user sentence |
| Results surface | 3.5-4.5 s | Skeleton rows waterfall about 0.06 s, then resolve about 0.2 s apart; lateral blur streak on a wrapper; turntable about 7 degrees per 4 s plus a 1.06 push | every row flips at once |
| Two-pane workspace | 4-5 s | Two nested camera nodes; z 12-22 px on touched elements; the list updates when the inbound item lands; turn about 6 degrees, push about 1.12 | |
| Pipeline tracker | 3-4 s | Real item photo and recipient; states about 0.18 s apart; evidence masonry | three checks floating on white (true and say nothing) |
| Hero stat | 3 s (5-7 s for two) | Start the count on the label word, not the spoken number; long counts linear; scale grows over about 0.9 s; at most a 2 px sine settle; about 2.5 percent dolly; projected figures labelled | |
| Testimonial | 3.5-4.5 s | Cite row (face, name and role, hairline, mark) at 0.04 s stagger; constant tilt; locked camera | |
| Comparison columns | 4.5-5.5 s | Table about 80 percent width, 5-8 rows in buyer language; partial coverage is the argument; staggers 0.1 / 0.15 / 0.24 s; the card squares up from about 6.5 degrees | strike-through or red crosses |
| End card | 3-4 s | Rhymes with the hook's register; the mark built twice (stroke draw then fill); brand-word sweep linear; idle ends about 0.3 s before the last frame; the last frame is a poster | tweening gradient stops; cutting the music dead |
| Hardware hero | 4-6 s | Use once; arrive already turning; text never in 3D; exploded view reassembles; hold at least 0.5 s; one move only (15-25 degree turntable or dolly) | |
| Terminal | 3-5 s | Reconstruct, never screen-record; mono 22-28 px at 1920; pre-laid-out rows; bursts 0.06-0.1 s; a determinate bar, never a spinner | a faked green test run |
| Map or chart | 3-4.5 s | Inline map, never live tiles; routes 0.4-0.6 s by dash offset, 3-5 hero routes; bars grow by scale from the axis, 0.08-0.12 s stagger | the chart plots numbers you do not have |

Never the same camera move on two adjacent shots; alternate busy and still (a 45 s film: about 3 dense, 4 still, 2 type frames); in single-take films no two consecutive beats resolve the same way.

## 4. Beat specification

Every beat specifies: start and end frame; the visual owner with asset ids; one primary action; its phases (anticipation, commit, impact, brake, settle); copy with a readable hold; colour state; in and out transitions; a "buys" sentence (the one belief the beat buys; if it needs "and", split it; if deleting it loses no belief, delete it); the importance level (1-3); and the strongest frame (hook at the first full line, turn at the brand pop, proof at the cause-effect frame, CTA at the slam or the cut before release).
