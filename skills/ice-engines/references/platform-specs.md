Platform limits and app chrome change often. Every number below is a recorded value with its source era (2026). Before final delivery, verify the target platform's current specs through dated research built from the brief's platform slot (see the ice-direction skill); when the live value differs, the live value wins and the decision log records it.

## 4. Re-layout per format

- Re-lay out; never crop a wide render into vertical. Write scenes against a layout function and render each ratio from the same timeline, reframing type and UI per format. Size type in container units (`cqw`, `cqh` with `container-type: size`) so it scales with the frame.
- Keep the payload in the central band (about 60%) so both 16:9 and 9:16 versions hold it; a 9:16 version is usually shorter (for example 45 s down to 30 s): cut a product-detail shot, never the claim.
- Create extra aspect ratios only when the brief asks; each is art-directed, not merely cropped.
- 9:16 only when asked or when the platform slot says so; do not assume an explainer is vertical.

## 5. Covers and first frames

- The first frame is the cover on many apps: the product name or title present as plain text from frame 0 (no entrance), important content inside the centered 1080x1080 square (y 420 to 1500 on 1080x1920), out of the watermark corner.
- Cover type sizes recorded at 1080 wide: title at least 170 px, other text at least 110 px, logo at least 200 px, at most 5 words on 2 lines, bold or black weight, contrast 4.5:1 or better, never over a face. Secondary on-screen text at least about 40 px at 1080 wide. Typography decisions: the ice-type skill.
- Arabic cover and thumbnail sizes: the `ice-arabic` skill, `references/covers-and-thumbnails.md` sections 2 and 3.
- Muted-first: assume the film plays without sound; the claim appears as type in the first seconds; burned captions plus an `.srt` built from the rendered timings (caption rules: the ice-type skill).
