# Art history: painter and movement styles (material layer)

Code-painted styles of named painters and movements that can be applied to a new subject. Read `style-method.md` first, especially section 9 (adapting a named tradition). Every implemented sample in the source shared one fixed demo room; that room is never part of a style.

Status labels are honest: "implemented, quality N stars" means a working single-scene renderer (3 stars recognizable at a glance, 2 recognizable with clear weaknesses, 1 redo first). Research recipes are in `art-history-eras.md` and are not implemented quality.

Related entries that live in other families: Chinese ink (`family-paint-wash.md`), 8-bit pixel (`family-games-toys.md`), vaporwave (`family-screen-tech-3d.md`), contemporary flat (`family-graphic-design.md`), Bauhaus and constructivism (`family-graphic-design.md`), early ray-traced CGI (`family-screen-tech-3d.md`).


## Shared rules for painter styles

- A named painter is a scope, not a template painting: learn brushwork, colour relations, light, form and space; never reproduce a specific painting's composition, figures or motifs. For living artists learn the medium only.
- Build in layers: a flat underpainting decides the composition; a style renderer repaints by sampling it (the renderer is "whose painting"); small objects on their own layer with finer settings; characters (fill, style texture, then clean contours and features); moving highlights and motif loops last.
- Keep stroke positions fixed between frames; let a direction field, light or colour vary continuously. Full-screen reseeding at 6-8 fps reads as flicker (measured frame differences of 18-20, larger than transitions).
- Regional palettes (7-8 hues per region) beat global jitter; mix 30-45 % of the underpainting value back in.
- Performance: cache static layers and redraw only moving parts; aim at or under about 150 ms per frame for the heaviest painter styles.

## Post-impressionism and impressionism

### Van Gogh post-impressionist flow strokes (`09_postimp`, implemented, quality 3 stars, the reference implementation)
- **Look:** thick directional strokes that follow a flow field per surface, dark-blue outlines, a dozen hues per region instead of one hue with noise; swirling skies and haloed lamps.
- **Medium-specific moves:** pipeline = flat base fill (walls, floor, window, big areas) -> flow-field stroke repaint with per-region palettes -> separate props layer (furniture, frames, lamps) with its own small strokes painted only onto existing pixels -> environment line art in dark blue 4.5px, re-seeded at 8fps -> figures (flat fill, fine strokes clipped to the figure, then lines and features) -> animated highlights. Direction field: walls vertical (-90 deg +/- 0.22 noise), floor along perspective lines toward the vanishing point, sky along the tangent of the nearest swirl plus 0.3t rotation, lamp halos along circles. Three stroke passes, never one: non-wall/non-floor cell 10 len 22 width 6.5; wall cell 14 len 64 width 8; floor cell 12 len 46 width 8; outline 0.8-0.9 mixing 40-45% dark blue; re-seed at 8fps. Motif loops: sky vortices turning, lamp halo dashed rings expanding, flame-like flicker of the direction field, concentric orange spiral bands turning at 0.6 rad/s clipped inside an animal's body. Signature transition: the starry vortex rolls the new picture in.
- **Palette:** wall `#7f9be6 #5f7fd8 #a7bbf2 #6c6fd2 #8ad0e6 #c3cbf6 #4f6cc8 #9a8fe0`; night sky `#22339a #2f4fb8 #5c86dc #8fb6ee #1b2a78 #3c6bd0`; floor `#c0573c #a8453a #d7774a #8e3b30 #5f8f6a #c96a52 #e08a5c`; region palettes mix 30-45% of the base colour.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Use when:** art-history speedruns, homage to 1889 painting, music videos needing turbulent energy, education on post-impressionism, any subject that should feel hand-painted and alive.
- **Do not use when:** frame budget is tight (heaviest style, about 110-120ms per frame); calm corporate explainers; small text or UI detail must stay legible.
- **Common mistakes:** one parameter set over the whole frame turns into carpet -> split walls, floor and the rest into three passes; small objects shattered by big strokes -> move them to a props layer; colour jitter alone gives "noisy single blue" -> use region palettes with 6-9 hues.
- **Known weakness:** most expensive renderer in the set (about 120ms per frame).
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/09_postimp.md` (MIT, translated).

### Impressionist pastel dabs, Monet 1874 (`08_impressionism`, implemented, quality 2 stars)
- **Look:** short pastel strokes in fixed positions over a flat underpainting, no outlines, warm sun patches on pink-violet walls, finer strokes for distant views, coloured light instead of black shadow.
- **Medium-specific moves:** flat base repainted each frame (moving flowers, clouds, plants), canvas pre-filled `#f2e6ee` so gaps show light ground. Two stroke passes: main frame cell 11 len 20 width 7 outline 0 colour jitter 26, masked away from distant views and inset pictures; distant views cell 6 len 11 width 4.5 inside the mask only (inset seascape uses horizontal water strokes). Direction field: wall slanting up-right (-0.95 rad in sunlit area, -0.55 elsewhere, +/-0.55 noise), floor near horizontal, curtains and cloth vertical, foliage radiating from the pot, sky horizontal. Figures: flat fill then strokes cell 7 len 13 clipped to the figure, features in thin lavender lines only. Stroke positions must be frozen (no re-seeding); motion comes from local motifs. Motif loops: 26 sun patches flickering irregularly (intensity 0.5+0.5 sin(t sp+ph) sin(0.53 t sp+2ph), clamped) drifting +/-44px, each 40 short yellow-white strokes; 60 diagonal light strokes on the sunlit wall at 6fps; poppies swaying with fixed stem bases (sin(3.2t+ph+0.02x) x (6+0.6r), later amplitude 10+); clouds moving 45px/s; ribbons fluttering. Signature transition: colour dabs tile across the frame until the new picture is covered.
- **Palette:** wall `#d8bede -> #f0c8cc -> #f2c4d4` plus r=900 warm-yellow radial sun; region palettes: wall 9 pink-violet-yellows, sunlit wall 7 cream-yellows, floor 7 rose-orange-violets, separate sky, grass, lavender curtain and blue-violet cloth sets (mix 0.3-0.45).
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Use when:** gentle lifestyle or garden subjects, spring or summer mood pieces, impressionism education, speedrun stop at 1874.
- **Do not use when:** faces and hands must read clearly in close-up (strokes break them up); budget below about 70ms per frame; hard-edged product shots.
- **Common mistakes:** a single coarse pass blurs distant houses and inset pictures -> separate fine pass for those areas; white window frames repainted wall colour -> treat colours above (240,236,225) as white; sun patches with screen blend at alpha 0.75 invisible on pink -> normal blend at alpha 0.95; whole-frame re-seed every 7-8 frames gave strobing (mean frame difference 4.14, peak 20.5) -> freeze stroke seeds with a per-cell hash so each cell consumes exactly one random number and never skips, result mean 0.57 peak 1.9 (original film 0.77); default dark-blue line colour leaves dark streaks beside faces even at line width 0 -> set the line colour to the hair or fur colour.
- **Known weakness:** faces and hands get broken up by strokes; two full-screen passes cost about 69ms per frame.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/08_impressionism.md` (MIT, translated).

### Seurat pointillism, optical colour mixing (`29_seurat`, implemented, quality 2 stars)
- **Look:** equal-size dots of pure pigment on a fixed hexagonal grid, colours mixed in the eye from pairs of palette colours, complementary sparks, light halos at edges, a dotted dark-blue painted border.
- **Medium-specific moves:** flat underpainting of the whole frame (about 3ms) laid down once blurred 4px, saturate 0.85, brightness 1.06 so gaps show a ground colour. Fixed hex grid pitch 10, row spacing 8.66, radius 4.5, static jitter +/-1.2px, about 24,000 dots batched into 21 paths (one fill per colour). Colour choice: for each palette pair project the target colour onto the segment between them, clamp the ratio to 0.2-0.8, add a penalty 0.004 x squared distance between the pair (stops grey being made from black and white), pick the closest pair, then choose either colour per dot by hash < ratio; cache results by 15-bit colour. Add +/-20 hashed jitter to the target before matching (R+d, G+0.8d, B-0.6d). 8% of dots swap to the complement (blue/orange, green/red-violet, yellow/violet; 21 mappings). Edge halo: compare with the pixel 14px down-right; if brightness differs by more than 45, 45% chance of a white dot on the light side and a dark-blue dot on the dark side. 28px dark-blue border also goes through the dot renderer. Motif loops: water dots re-hashed at 6fps as glitter; 7% of dots switch to their pair colour at 6fps so light trembles; small drifting boats at 40px/s; a fluttering insect path sin(2.6t) x 120. Signature transition: the picture crystallises into dots (pitch 14; dots grow to r 6.4 in the first 30%, old picture fades to paper under them; each dot switches colour at 0.5(0.6x/W+0.4y/H)+0.25 hash+0.15 with a 1.35x pop; new image develops under the dots after p 0.6, dots retract after p 0.7; needs pixel reads of both frames, about 10ms).
- **Palette:** 14 saturated `#1a2058 #2a3a9a #3f6fd0 #6aaee6 #1f6a52 #2f9a5a #a8c83a #f2cf3a #f08a2a #e0432a #c0306a #7a4aa8 #f8f4e8 #e8b48a` plus 7 tints mixed with white `#f6e49a #f6c48a #cfe08a #b0cff0 #f2b8c8 #c99a4a #9a7ac8`.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Use when:** calm leisure scenes, science-of-colour education, data or product shots wanting a crafted texture, 1884 speedrun stop.
- **Do not use when:** strong motion or action beats are needed (the style is static); fine text must be read; budget below about 100ms per frame.
- **Common mistakes:** favouring single pure colours (penalty term) cuts gradients into vertical bands -> pair matching; continuous ratios still switch whole columns at boundaries -> add hashed target jitter before matching; palette without tints turns light walls into "snow noise" -> add the 7 white-mixed tints; 4fps with 3% swaps judged frozen (adjacent-frame difference 0.1-0.2) -> 6fps with 7% swaps (0.45).
- **Known weakness:** reads as static; the grid looks too regular.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/29_seurat.md` (MIT, translated).

## Light and drama

### Rembrandt chiaroscuro (`32_rembrandt`, implemented, quality 1 star)
- **Look:** near-black warm ambience, figures carved out of darkness by a few light pools, lost edges on the shadow side, impasto only on lit highlights, golden-brown glaze, craquelure.
- **Medium-specific moves:** paint the scene at full brightness, repaint background with broad brown strokes (cell 18, len 54, width 16, jitter 6, angle -0.9+1.6 noise; palette mix 0.7) then blur 2.2px at 75%; figures flat plus volume gradients plus fine strokes; lost edges = figure layer blurred 2.4px opaque plus a sharp copy at alpha 0.62. Then build a light map on base rgb(30,21,13) (ambient 0.13) by additively adding blurred (40px) shaft wedge alpha 0.8-0.95, window r 330, face pool r 250 alpha 0.95, chest r 230 alpha 0.55, table r 280 alpha 0.6, candle r 190+40fc alpha 0.6+0.4fc, and a separation light on the wall behind the dark side (r 300 alpha 0.35); multiply the whole frame by it. Impasto bristle stroke: round(w/1.3) parallel bristles, brightness +/-17, ends trimmed 0-25%, width 1.4-2.6, shadow line alpha 0.28 below, 2+w/3 light specks above, only on lit hair, collars, porcelain, pearls. Overlay: craquelure 0.28, grain density 0.05 alpha 0.12, vignette 0.75, soft-light rgba(200,140,60,0.35). Motif loops: candle flicker flick(t) = 0.5 noise(2.2t)+0.25 noise(6.5t), light pools drift +/-14px; cloud shadow dims window light x(1-0.35 cloud), cloud = 0.5+0.5 sin(2.2t-0.6), dark blob crossing glass at 260px/s; 70 dust motes visible only inside the shaft. Signature transition: INDEX lists an old-film iris close and open; the transition tests assign that iris to the 1930s cartoon, so a candle-pool reveal from darkness is the safer native process.
- **Palette:** ambient rgb(30,21,13); glaze rgba(200,140,60,0.35); browns and golds.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Use when:** dramatic portraits, heritage and museum films, moody product reveals, education on Baroque light.
- **Do not use when:** bright cheerful brands; information-dense frames (most of the frame is dark); flat graphic subjects.
- **Common mistakes:** impasto as three tidy lines (shadow, body, ridge) reads as white plasters -> bristle strokes; copying regions of the original back pulls unstroked background as rectangular patches -> draw props as their own layer after strokes; grain 0.12 / alpha 0.22 dirties faces -> 0.05 / 0.12; hand drawn after the held vessel covers it -> draw hand, then vessel, then thumb.
- **Known weakness:** figures flat (review 5/10); the style most in need of a redo.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/32_rembrandt.md` (MIT, translated).

### Contemporary anime cinematic light (cumulus, god rays, cel shading) (`35_anime_cinematic_light`, implemented, quality 2 stars)
- **Look:** Inspired by Makoto Shinkai. Photoreal-leaning painted interiors and skies, towering cumulus with sunlit cores, Tyndall light shafts with floating dust, lens flare and bloom, crisp cel shadows plus rim light on figures, warm-cool split toning. Reference grammar only: never reproduce named characters, designs or compositions; scope one film or period first.
- **Medium-specific moves:** cel function: shadow = part minus the part shifted along the light (even-odd clip of a big rectangle plus the shifted path), rim = part minus the part shifted against the light by rw; light from the window lx=-1 ly=-0.55 so shadow falls lower-right; shift sd 22-30 big parts, 10 faces, 8 small parts; rw 3-9; line `#6a4632` 2.2px; order fill -> shadow -> rim -> line per part (correct occlusion without hidden-line logic). Back-lit subjects: lx 0.5 ly 1 plus an additive warm rim rgba(255,220,160,0.55). Anime eye: white 8x12, iris vertical gradient `#1f3f7a -> #3f7fd0 -> #9fd4ff`, two highlights, 3.4px upper lid plus one corner lash; hair halo arc 9px rgba(255,250,226,0.85). Cumulus: 70+12 puffs drawn as one blue-grey silhouette (`#b7c6e6 -> #8297c6`), each puff gets a white radial ball of 0.82r offset (+0.22r, -0.3r) toward the sun, whole layer blurred 3px then 55% of the sharp layer on top; radius breathes 0.05 sin(1.3t), drift 10px/s. Flare: sun core r 130, 10 rays length 90/170 turning 0.25 rad/s, horizontal streak 840x4, 5 hexagon ghosts on the sun-to-centre line at 0.45/0.75/1.25/1.6/2.0 alpha 0.04-0.12. Tyndall: 3 wedges blurred 18px, cached, screen at alpha 0.75+0.25 sin(3.4t), 60 dust motes. Bloom: downscale to 480x270, blur 6px, brightness 1.1, screen alpha 0.28. Motif loops: clouds churning and drifting, contrails, birds flapping, a water drop sliding down glass with a trail, sheer curtain and its moving shadow, leaves and hair in wind, rotating flare rays, pulsing shafts. Signature transition: diagonal light sweep (slanted boundary k=0.35 from -500 to W+700 eased; new picture left, old right; three additive light bands 520px alpha 0.55 warm, 160px alpha 0.85 white, 60px alpha 0.6 cyan; plus streak, 40 light particles, 3 hexagon ghosts).
- **Palette:** skin `#ffe8dc / #f2b6a8`; blonde `#f8d681 / #d8a050`; white uniform `#fbfbfe / #b9c3dc`; navy `#2c3a66 / #1b2446`; cloud shadow `#b7c6e6 -> #8297c6`.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium (piano and strings suit the genre).
- **Use when:** emotional youth or travel films, summer skies, city-light mood pieces, education on light and atmosphere, brand films about aspiration.
- **Do not use when:** interiors need dense detail (backgrounds are thin); flat graphic brands; anything that must not look like a specific anime film.
- **Common mistakes:** sun placed on a window frame corner spills flare onto the wall like a lamp -> keep the sun inside the glass, flare radius 130 or less; clouds as individual radial gradients with lit arcs read as soap bubbles -> silhouette plus offset light ball plus blurred and sharp layers; a 1800px horizontal streak looks like a bug line -> 840px alpha 0.35 near the sun; solid water drops look like floating bubbles -> lower arc only, alpha 0.22 body and one highlight; duplicate variable names made the scene fail to load and silently fall back to a placeholder -> check scene load errors.
- **Known weakness:** interior background density is not high enough. About 71ms per frame.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/35_shinkai.md` (MIT, translated).

### Hand-painted anime watercolour background (`25_painterly_anime_background`, implemented background only, quality 2 stars; whole-film visual language is research)
- **Look:** Inspired by Studio Ghibli backgrounds. Bright transparent watercolour washes with pigment-pooled edges, large soft colour blooms, opaque white clouds, figures in two-tone cel shading with a single crescent shadow and warm brown line; environment motion always has a cause (wind, gravity, contact). Reference grammar only: never reproduce named characters, designs or compositions; scope one film or period first (the source warns that a later hand-drawn-line film by the same studio follows a different line and blank-space logic and must not be merged into this watercolour recipe).
- **Medium-specific moves:** wash recipe: deform a polygon by midpoint displacement (push each edge midpoint along its normal by a near-Gaussian amount, sum of 3 uniforms, scaled by min(1, len/80), amplitude x0.6 per round, 3 base rounds); each layer deforms 2 more rounds and fills with multiply at low alpha, plus a 1.6px same-colour edge stroke at edge alpha, so stacked edges form the water-stain rim. Typical: big walls 5 layers alpha 0.4 amplitude 12; colour blooms 3 layers alpha 0.045 amplitude 40; boards 3 layers alpha 0.5 amplitude 3 edge 0.14; opaque whites normal blend alpha 0.9-0.95. Walls = radial-gradient wash plus 28 large deformed blooms and corner darkening; paper grain multiply alpha 0.3. Cel figure: fill shadow colour, then inside the figure clip fill the base colour shifted toward the light, leaving a back-lit crescent; 2.4px line `#4a3428` (softened to `#6a4a38`); then multiply a grain-plus-40-blooms texture (alpha 0.1, amplitude 16) cut to the figure silhouette so figures match the paper. Motif loops: curtains billow q^2 x (60+150 gust), gust = 0.55+0.45 sin(5.4 lt+phase), plus small sin(7lt+6q) ripples with fixed seeds; grass blades swayed by travelling wind 0.5+0.5 sin(3.1t-0.018x); clouds drift 22-30px/s; tree crown rocks +/-0.025 rad around its root; 34 dappled light spots (screen, warm yellow, noise drift, brightness sin(5t+1.7i)). Signature transition: wet paper bleed (5 colour blooms open in sequence, radius q x 1400 x k with boundary R(1+0.28 fbm(angle)+0.05 sin 9a); 70px blurred bright halo outside where the water arrives first, new picture inside, 5px multiply brown water-stain rim; 0.38s, about 127ms per frame).
- **Palette:** wall light `#fbf3e0` to corner `#dcc49a`; blooms alternate warm yellow, pink, blue-grey, pale green; line `#4a3428` / `#6a4a38`.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Use when:** warm everyday-life subjects, homage to hand-painted Japanese animation backgrounds, gentle education or brand films about home, craft or neighbourhood; the source plans transfer tests on non-forest settings (laundromat, irrigation, spacecraft repair).
- **Do not use when:** you need studio-level character acting (figures are the weak part); "everyday feel" is being equated with slow motion only; dark gritty subjects.
- **Common mistakes:** blooms alpha 0.07 x 4 layers with grain multiply 0.5 made the wall look like mouldy parchment -> 0.045 x 3 layers and lift the gradient one step; two rectangular washes left a hard seam at x about 1100 -> darken with heavily deformed polygons (amplitude 60), never rectangles; figures read as flat vector next to watercolour -> apply the silhouette-cut watercolour texture; curtains not visibly moving -> gust frequency 3.2 to 5.4 and billow q^2(60+150 gust); first frame cost 619ms -> cache the watercolour base at boot.
- **Known weakness:** figures weaker than backgrounds; the newer single-scene study still reads as programmatic illustration, not studio-film level, and has no cross-scene validation.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/25_ghibli.md` (MIT, translated).

## Modernism and pop

### Analytic cubism, Picasso / Braque 1912 (`11_cubism`, implemented, quality 2 stars)
- **Look:** translucent overlapping planes each lit light-to-dark, passage lines running through, brown-grey-ochre monochrome, stencil letters, newspaper scraps, faux woodgrain, figures broken into large faceted planes spilling past their contours, profile plus frontal eye. Picasso and Braque works remain under copyright in places: learn the language, never copy a specific canvas.
- **Medium-specific moves:** plane = polygon with a linear gradient from vertex 0 to a random vertex, stops 25% cream mix -> base -> 40% dark-brown mix, alpha 0.18-0.7; stack about 18 large planes, 9 radial fan wedges alternating light and dark (alpha 0.3) and 70 random 3-4 sided shards. Texture: dotted patches (9px diamond grid r 1.6), hatch patches (7px diagonals), 9,000 tiny 3-8x1.5px dashes (55% cream alpha 0.28, 45% brown alpha 0.16), 46 thin through-lines 1.3px alpha 0.55, comb woodgrain with Gaussian bumps at knots. Figures: render flat off-screen, triangulate on a jittered grid, fill each triangle with its centroid colour x(0.8+0.38 rand) plus a light-to-dark gradient, and do not clip triangles to the silhouette (planes overflow the contour); cell 58 for bodies, 26 for small subjects; re-jitter vertices +/-8% at 8fps and let 16-35% of facets flicker +/-12% brightness. Re-add structure afterwards (face planes, frontal eye, outlines as straight segments overshooting 10-16px each end) and lay 5 alpha 0.2 planes over figures sliding sin(6lt) x 8 to embed them. Motif loops: stencil word letters swapping order at 12fps (y +/-13, rotation +/-0.11 rad, gaps cut out), newspaper scrap trembling at 8fps, window panes showing one view at different offsets (dx = sin(9lt+1.7i) x 18) with a translucent triangle sliding at 260px/s. Signature transition: geometric shards slide out of register and reassemble into the new picture.
- **Palette:** paper `#d6c8ac`, cream `#ece3cf`, ochre `#b8975f`, brown `#6e5538`, dark brown `#3a2c1e`, grey `#a8a395`, blue-grey `#8e9eab`, dark grey `#5a5852`; view `#c9d3da #8fa3b3 #e4e6e2 #6f8597 #b4c1cb #5f7385`.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Use when:** multi-viewpoint explainers, art education, intellectual or editorial pieces, 1912 speedrun stop.
- **Do not use when:** faces must be recognisable; bright saturated brands; simple single-message ads.
- **Common mistakes:** first frame differed from later frames (GPU vs CPU readback and leftover line-join state) -> create readback layers with frequent-read flag and fully reset the context each frame; cell 34 facets look like low-poly 3D -> cell 58; hair re-facet with a round clip turned half the face gold -> filter by colour; sleeves same value as skirt merge -> sleeves darker.
- **Known weakness:** too bright, lacks large dark-brown planes; contours still realistic curves.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/11_cubism.md` (MIT, translated).

### Matisse paper cut-outs (`30_matisse`, implemented, quality 3 stars)
- **Look:** saturated flat gouache-painted paper shapes with scissor-cut irregular edges, pinned to the wall with soft drop shadows, seaweed and star motifs, cobalt blue checkerboards. Matisse works remain under copyright in places: learn the medium, do not recreate specific compositions.
- **Medium-specific moves:** scissor edge: sample the outline every 14-24px, jitter +/-2-2.5px with a seed, join with straight segments. Seaweed motif: tapered stem plus 2 x lobes alternating round-ended capsule fingers (angle +/-(1.05-0.55u), length width(1-0.45u), thickness width(0.34-0.12u)) all added to one path and filled once (union, one shadow); never modulate width along a spine. Figures without point lists: displace the figure layer per pixel with a static fbm field (0.028 frequency, amplitude 9) to rough the edges (about 15ms). Pinned-paper shadow offset (5,6) blur 4 rgba(10,20,40,0.3). Gouache streaks: 2,600 cached strokes 60-300px long, width 6-22, half black half white, alpha 0.04-0.13, tilt -0.25 rad, overlay blend. Motif loops: seaweed swings about its root sin(2.8t+phase) x 0.13 with scaleX 0.93-1.0 breathing; stars spin and pulse 0.82+0.22 sin(7t+1.9k); heart beat 1+0.18 max(0, sin 9t); birds flap scaleY 0.55+0.45 cos 13t; a paper scrap tumbles (scaleX = cos 6t). Signature transition: a 1100px cobalt paper with sine-cut edges sweeps right to left (shadow (14,10) blur 12), carrying 260 gouache streaks and pinned seaweed, stars and discs; the new picture is behind its trailing edge.
- **Palette:** cobalt `#1d4fb8`, light blue `#4f95dc`, emerald `#1f9a5a`, black, yellow, red, pink, white.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium (jazz suits the series it echoes).
- **Use when:** joyful brand films, children's education, music videos, kinetic posters, anything wanting handmade graphic warmth.
- **Do not use when:** realism or detailed faces matter; dark or serious subjects; dense data.
- **Common mistakes:** seaweed by width modulation along a spine looked like caterpillars and zig-zag lightning -> capsule-finger union; falling scraps crossing a face -> route them away from focal faces; dark text on cobalt unreadable -> back it with a white paper scrap.
- **Known weakness:** gouache streaks a little heavy.
- **Source:** alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion `references/风格配方/30_matisse.md` (MIT, translated).

### Lichtenstein comic pop with Warhol colourway grid (`13_pop`, implemented, quality 3 stars)
- **Look:** Ben-Day dots, 5px heavy black outlines over flat fills, white highlight dashes, hatched shadows, sculptural blonde curls with black C-arcs, burst balloons and onomatopoeia, four-panel colourway swaps.
- **Medium-specific moves:** Ben-Day dots as a repeating tile (pitch x pitch tile with 4 corner dots plus one centre dot = 45 deg grid, diagonal spacing pitch/sqrt 2) used as fill, aligned to screen space so dots do not swim with moving shapes: wall pitch 24 r 5.6 blue on white (17px diagonal period); skin pitch 13 r 3.3 red on skin; floor pitch 14 r 2.2 alpha 0.18 faint print dots. Everything = black 5px stroke plus flat fill; shadows as 10px-spaced 3.5px black diagonals inside an ellipse clip. Union outlines: stroke all shapes 10-11px black first, then fill all. Motif loops: four-panel colourway rotation step = floor(lt x 11) (about every 5.5 frames), panel i uses set (i+step) mod 4; thought-bubble dots flash in sequence on a 12-frame cycle (dot k lit when (f-2k) mod 12 < 4, r 20 and up 6px, else r 12); burst panel jitter at 30fps +/-8/+/-6px +/-0.03 rad with 10 black action spikes pulsing 0.85+0.25 sin(40lt+2i); a crouching body wiggle at 6Hz amplitude 16 weighted toward the rear; steam as two black wavy lines jittering at 14Hz. Signature transition: yellow lightning bolt plus halftoning (0.16s).
- **Palette:** black `#141414`; paper `#f3eedb`; blue `#2f5cc8`; yellow `#f7d52c`; red `#d8232a` / `#a8161c`; white `#fbf8ee`; skin `#fae0cc` with dots `#e2483c`; blonde `#f8d62c` / `#d9a514`; colourway grounds `#9ccf3a #e0359a #2fc0b0 #f08a2a`.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
