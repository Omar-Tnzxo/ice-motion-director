# Family: print, paper and craft

Printmaking, cut paper, collage, puppetry, glass and thread. Read `style-method.md` first. Every entry's examples are illustration only.


## Shared rules for this family

- A print is pulled, not drawn: each ink or block arrives as its own pass (a nip line, a baren pull, a squeegee stroke), lightest first, key plate last, with a breath between passes. Registration is good but never perfect: each plate keeps its own small offset.
- White is bare paper, never a pale ink. Tone comes from the process (halftone dot area, bokashi wipe, hatch density, overprint), not from gradients.
- Paper moves; ink never moves once printed. Paper pieces are held above the stack, lowered and pressed, and their shadow tightens as they land. Stop-motion pieces step on twos with a small damped settle.
- Depth in paper styles is shadow and gap, never faked 3D. Animating a cut shape's size re-cuts its edge every frame (shimmer): scale with the camera or a transform instead.
- Every object, character or scene this family draws in code follows `code-illustration-craft.md` (three tones under one light, cast and contact shadows, a texture layer, one line policy, named groups); section 8 there says how this family renders each rule.
- **Craft profile (family default):** needs: finish (the substrate and the process mark: paper fibre, ink spread and a small registration offset per plate, or the glass, hide or thread of those entries; white is the bare substrate and tone comes from the process, never a gradient, rules above and `code-illustration-craft.md` section 8 row "Print, paper and craft"; an entry whose Look has flat plates with no modelled form lifts `art:shading`), rhythm (pass by pass: each ink, block or piece arrives as its own pull or placement, lightest first and key last, a breath between, cut pieces stepping on twos with a damped settle, rules above), transition (made from the process: a pull, a press, a piece lowered, a sheet thrown or a scroll slid; never a fade, `style-method.md` section 6), foley (the process sound: press, drum, baren, scissors, paper, rods or thread, `style-method.md` section 12); serves when: depth (near and far, or stacked pieces: scoped: layers, mist bands, shadow and gap, never faked 3D, rules above), composite (paper, puppet or cut pieces: a cast shadow per piece that tightens as it lands, rules above), light (a lit object, or a lightbox, lamp or glass entry: one key with contact shadows, or the light passing through the material), camera (a sheet larger than the frame or several sheets: a scroll slide, a slow push to a framing device, a pull back from one sheet to many), type (words on screen: printed, stamped or cut as part of the sheet, never floating type), data (a number or a sequence: printed into the sheet in the process's own marks), silence (a reveal or the key line: the press stops), music (music in the type or the brief: the entry's instruments, `style-method.md` section 12).

## Risograph (merged: two sources)

Both sources agree: one drum per ink, every other colour an overprint, white is paper. The hand variant shows the print being fed drum by drum with only the nip line moving; the film variant uses riso as a living world with misregistration that lags fast moves.

### Risograph Print (`risograph`)
- **Look:** Animation that looks printed frame by frame on a stencil duplicator: 2-3 translucent spot inks overprinted (multiplied) on warm off-white paper, halftone dots per plate on its own screen angle, ink grain, pinholes, feed streaks, plates a few px out of register; flat shapes with no outlines. Not flat vector with a noise overlay, not CMYK pop-art halftone (no process colours, no keyline), not opaque screen-print; never show a duplicator brand, logo or machine trade dress.
- **Medium-specific moves:** characters on twos (12 fps), camera on ones (24 fps); registration fixed per shot (+-1-3 px), a kick of ~8-14 px decaying in ~0.25 s with slight ringing on accents, a drift up to ~25 px through a held moment snapping back on the downbeat; grain boils partly (about a third re-rolls per print), streaks and blots change only at cuts; halftone period ~6-8 px at 1080p, densities >= ~0.9 print solid, screen angles >= 15 deg apart; dots anchored to the paper so a pan slides the image under a fixed screen; a plate can arrive mid-shot with a roller sweep (moving edge, heavier ink band); characters carry a 4-5 px paper-white knockout halo that picks up coloured fringes; simple cycles, secondary motion as a travelling sine.
- Camera list: wide lateral tracking with a small figure and empty sky; static wide with the subject crossing; locked horizon-disc-figure composition; ground-level low angle with big foreground shapes; medium two-shot held for a look; straight top-down with long shadows; push into a halftone field until dots are shapes; pull back to reveal the paper; match cut on a flat shape. Transitions: overprint growths, roller sweeps, shape match cuts, hard cuts on beats; never cross-dissolve or soft digital wipe.
- **Palette:** 2-3 spot inks, never more, plus warm paper (~`#F4EEE2`, never pure white); every other colour is an overprint or a tint; no gradients except stepped halftone bands (e.g. 20/40/60/100 %); each area uses at most two plates with at most one as a tint; design for the one-plate version (every main colour contains the first plate); skin is a light tint of the palest ink; save full overprint for the earned moment. Sets: `#0078BF` + `#FFE800` + `#FF48B0`; `#00838A` + `#FF7477`; `#3D5588` + `#FF665E` + `#FFB511`.
- **Type and captions:** Type is printed on one plate and misregisters with it; a title can double-hit on two plates with visible offset. OFL heavy grotesque for titles (Bricolage Grotesque 800), geometric sans for captions (Jost 500-700); preload every weight. Subtitles on a paper-white knockout strip with text on one plate, ~40-46 px; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Prints never fade: titles arrive by roller wipe or on a beat and leave on a cut.
- **Sound:** Warm, dry, handmade, slightly lo-fi: upright or electric bass, vibraphone or marimba, nylon guitar, whistled or hummed melody, electric piano, toy piano, brushed kit, shaker and rim clave, small drum machine through tape saturation; option: one instrument per plate entering and leaving. Signature foley is the machine: feed "shhk", drum "ka-chunk" on a plate arrival, motor hum, sheet landing on a stack, guillotine cut; other foley dry, close and papery. Silence is a held bar with tails cut, paired with a registration drift. Dry close mix, music ducked ~8 dB, -14 LUFS, relaxed conversational voice, no grain added at mux.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: finish (2-3 translucent spot inks multiplied on warm off-white paper, a halftone per plate on its own screen angle anchored to the paper, ink grain, pinholes and feed streaks, a paper-white knockout halo round characters; registration fixed per shot, grain partly re-rolled per print, streaks changing only at cuts; no grain added at mux, entry Look, moves and Sound), rhythm (characters on twos and the camera on ones; a registration kick of about 8-14 px decaying in about 0.25 s on accents, a drift through a held moment snapping back on the downbeat, a plate arriving mid-shot with a roller sweep, entry moves), transition (overprint growths, roller sweeps, shape match cuts and hard cuts on beats; never a cross-dissolve or a soft digital wipe, entry camera list); serves when also: silence (a held moment: a bar with its tails cut, paired with a registration drift, entry Sound), voice (a narrator: a relaxed, conversational voice, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** playful everyday comedy of scale, routines and commutes, mergers and overlaps (two inks make a third), eras or turning points added as plates, zines and poster series, indie products, cultural events, data whose overlap is the finding.
- **Do not use when:** the brand needs exact colour fidelity or photoreal imagery; more than three colours are mandatory; a solemn or luxury register where grain and misregistration read as cheap; dense small text that halftone and misregistration would blur.
- **Common mistakes:** three halftoned plates in one area become mud -> two plates, one tint; a poor hash draws stripes across solids at 1080p -> a well-mixed hash; per-frame registration jitter and streaks flicker and bloat files -> fixed offsets per shot, kicks on accents, sheet noise at cuts, partial boil; lossy chroma-subsampled frames smear dots -> lossless frames into a 4:4:4 intermediate, check moire at 100 % and 50 %; halo layer not copying the transform misplaces zoomed subjects -> share the transform; a main colour lacking the first plate floats in one-ink frames -> build recipes up from the one-ink silhouette; even-odd arches fill solid -> trace inner edges in the opposite direction.
- **Variation slots:** structure: two inks in dialogue that only overlap at the end, or subtraction losing a plate per loss until one ink remains (illustration only); opening: a knockout shape filled by its plate seconds later, or extreme halftone close-up pulling back (illustration only); ending: plates lift away until one ink stands, or the print becomes a poster pasted inside its own world (illustration only).
- **Source:** lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar `styles/risograph/STYLE.md` (MIT; demo media CC BY 4.0 LemoLab).

### Risograph, drum by drum (`lighthouse`, hand)
- **Look:** soy ink, one layer per drum multiplied through its own registration offset; every other colour is an overprint; tone is a halftone screen per ink angle; white is bare paper.
- **Medium-specific moves:** a riso print is fed, not drawn: the sheet goes through once per ink; each ink lands at the nip line, a straight front travelling down the sheet at constant feed speed with a short grip and release and the drum's shadow riding just ahead; a breath while the drum is swapped; lightest ink first, key plate last (shadow sides and key lines arrive in one sweep); every overprint is born at the nip line. The only moving thing is the nip line; never a fade.
- **Palette:** 2-3 fluorescent or bright soy inks plus their overprints.
- **Sound:** machine feed rhythm, drum swap clunk; lo-fi or indie electronic.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: rhythm (the nip line is the only moving thing: one straight front per ink at constant feed speed, a breath while the drum is swapped, entry moves), finish (tone is a halftone screen per ink angle and every other colour an overprint of the drums, entry Look), foley (the machine's feed rhythm and a drum swap clunk, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** indie brands, zines, posters, cultural events, loud social pieces.
- **Do not use when:** photographic realism; corporate finance sobriety.
- **Common mistakes:** fades or slides -> the nip line only; mixing colours digitally -> only overprints of the drums.
- **Source:** alexgreensh/anidoodle `references/styles/lighthouseDraw.md`, `references/styles.md` (Apache-2.0).

## Halftone (merged: two sources)

Dots only, area carries tone. The dossier variant is a loud stamped case-file look for humour and social shorts; the newsprint variant is a sober two-plate press print of a photograph for news and history.

### Halftone Dossier (`halftone-dossier`)
- **Look:** A retro print case file: warm cream archive paper with mottling, grain, vignette and fold; shading only as overprinted halftone dots in a few flat inks; huge heavy headlines with misregistered shadows; rubber stamps that slam; thick-outlined chunky characters; bureaucratic apparatus (case numbers, exhibits, height charts, name plates). Not a two-ink riso zine, not grid-driven Swiss kinetic type, not candy game-show flat; dots, paper and stamps visible in every frame.
- **Medium-specific moves:** 30 fps transforms, line boil ~10 fps on characters and hand-drawn props only (never text, dots, HUD or captions; ~5 px displacement); everything enters with overshoot (back ease 0.3-0.45 s), rising from below or scaling from 0, nothing fades in; per-character headline pop (each glyph drops 30-100 px and squashes, stagger 0.05-0.08 s, 0.02 s for small text); stamp slam ~0.09 s from ~2.6x to 1x then damped bounce with shake, flash and sound on the same frame; volume-preserving squash landings; idle life (breathing, blinks, blinking REC dot); halftone step 20-26 px, radius = density x step x 0.6-0.72, layers at different screen angles multiplied; dot-wipe transitions (~0.44 s, diagonal delay, scene switches while covered); hard cut saved for a big hit; one idea per 2-4 s card.
- Camera list: locked frontal with ~0.6 % beat pulse; slow push-in 1 to 1.05-1.08; shake with quadratic decay 5-26 px; flash frames capped at 0.5 for big hits; pan across a pinned evidence board; snap zoom into a halftone detail; page turn or folder slide; pull back to reveal the whole desk. Compose like a page: headline top-left, subject right-centre, caption bar bottom-centre, HUD in corners; no dissolves.
- **Palette:** Paper ground, one dark ink (navy, black-brown or deep green) for outlines, body text, caption bar and fades, plus 4-5 flat spot inks: one overprint-shadow ink, one highlight ink for keywords and numerals, one warm red reserved for stamps and alarms; no gradients or transparency ramps; secret or night scenes switch to a dark-ink ground with a lighter dot layer; at most five inks plus paper. Sets: cream / navy / cobalt / hot pink / yellow / red; manila / sepia-black / teal / mustard / vermilion; off-white / forest green / tomato / sky / ochre.
- **Type and captions:** (font names illustration only; choose the families per the ice-direction skill, `references/type-pairing.md`) Headlines in a black-weight serif (Noto Serif SC 900 for CJK, a black slab for Latin) 100-230 px, left-aligned, with a +8/+8 px misregistration shadow; fat rounded display (Bagel Fat One) for names and numbers; playful hand face (ZCOOL KuaiLe) for interjections; heavy mono (JetBrains Mono 800) for case numbers and rulers; black sans for captions and stamps. Cards are the subtitles: a dark-ink rounded pill with an offset shadow, paper text, keyword in highlight ink, popping after the headline, hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). HUD: case number and chapter chip top-left, REC dot and date top-right. Latin runs 2-3x longer: smaller headlines, 1-2 word stamps.
- **Sound:** Synth pop-print kit: synth kick, snare, clap, hat, square-ish 8th-note bass, triangle pad, marimba-like pluck (fundamental plus ~4x partial), pizzicato and finger snaps for sneaking, music-box bell for tenderness. Foley: stamp as pitch-dropping boom plus click (crackle for big ones), camera shutter, paper slides, folder flaps, typewriter clicks, whooshes into wipes, pops per glyph, boings and plops, synthesized creature voices. Empty the music before a verdict so the stamp lands alone. Voice optional, dry deadpan report. SFX ahead of music on impacts, soft-clip, -14 LUFS, true peak <= -1 dB.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: finish (warm cream archive paper with mottling, grain, vignette and a fold; lifts `slop:N11` for that vignette; shading only as overprinted halftone dots of flat inks at different screen angles; line boil about 10 fps on characters and drawn props only, never on text, dots or captions, entry Look and moves), rhythm (everything enters with overshoot, rising from below or scaling from 0, nothing fading in; per-character headline pops, stamp slams of about 0.09 s with a damped bounce, shake, flash and sound on the same frame; one idea per 2-4 s card, entry moves), type (huge heavy headlines with a misregistered shadow, cards as the subtitles, a HUD of case number and chapter chip top-left and a REC dot and date top-right; lifts `lint:C1`, `lint:C3`, `slop:N1` and `slop:N2` for that HUD and `lint:T1` for the per-character headline pops, entry Look, moves and Type and captions), transition (dot wipes of about 0.44 s with a diagonal delay, a hard cut saved for a big hit; no dissolves, entry moves and camera list), camera (locked frontal with a small beat pulse, slow push-ins, shake with quadratic decay on hits, snap zooms into a halftone detail; flash frames capped at 0.5, entry camera list), sfx (stamps as a pitch-dropping boom plus click, whooshes into wipes, pops per glyph, boings and plops, entry Sound); serves when also: silence (a verdict: the music empties so the stamp lands alone, entry Sound), voice (a narrator: optional, a dry deadpan report, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** explainers framed as "the case of X", myth-busting and fact checks, ranked causes and numbered lists, product teardowns as mugshots, timelines and supply chains on an evidence board, approvals and verdicts, playful compliance or policy content, reveals via redaction.
- **Do not use when:** the brand is calm, premium or minimal; the content is genuinely sensitive (real crime, victims) where a farcical case-file frame trivializes; long-form narration without card-sized ideas; photographic or realistic product needs.
- **Common mistakes:** fonts not loaded before layout overlap glyphs -> preload every family, block font display; boil on text or dots turns them to mush -> boil only characters and props; shared clip ids collide -> unique id per clipped numeral; scene elements visible before their cue -> explicit opacity 0 before start; dot wipe short of full cover shows the switch -> max radius covers the cell diagonal; 100 % white flash reads as a dropped frame -> cap ~0.5; dots under ~14 px step alias to moire -> keep step larger; too many inks lose the print feel -> at most five plus paper.
- **Variation slots:** structure: a report unredacted page by page, or a file worked backwards from the last event, or exhibits contradicting a statement (illustration only); opening: one object on a dark-ink ground with the file assembling around it, or a caption bar alone before the file drops in (illustration only); ending: the file goes into a drawer of identical files, or the final answer stays redacted (illustration only).
- **Source:** lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar `styles/halftone-dossier/STYLE.md` (MIT; demo media CC BY 4.0 LemoLab).

### Newsprint halftone (`halftone`, hand)
- **Look:** a web-offset newspaper print of a photograph: an AM halftone where dot AREA carries tone; two plates only: black on the 45 degree screen and one warm orange spot at 15 degrees (30 degrees apart so they rosette, never moire); oil ink gaining into unbleached groundwood newsprint.
- **Medium-specific moves:** only dots, no line: every edge is where dot size changes; dots area-true (radius from sqrt of tone over pi), swelling past 70 % into solids with pinholes, gaining most in midtones, each dot an irregular bitten heptagon with a soft spread halo; highlights under about 4 % drop out, shadows plug; the spot plate laid a hair wide and off register (a thin orange lip at dark edges). Build the original continuous-tone picture first, then screen each separation. Motion (make-ready): sheets thrown on a delivery pile on each beat (lands in 6 frames), the spot unit comes up to colour first, streaky and uneven, then the black unit, then the spot nudged into register; the final sheet is the still.
- **Palette:** black `#1d1a17`, spot orange `#e86a2c`, newsprint `#e6dfcc`. The spot is a warmth, not a colour.
- **Sound:** press rumble, sheet slaps, paper; newsroom ambience.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: finish (tone only by dot area on two plates, black and one spot, with no line anywhere; lifts `art:tones`, entry Look and moves), rhythm (make-ready: a sheet thrown on the pile on each beat, the spot unit first, then black, then the spot nudged into register, entry moves), ambience (newsroom ambience under the press, entry Sound); serves when also: footage (a real photograph of the subject: it is the continuous-tone original screened into the plates, entry moves).
- **Use when:** news, history, retro editorial, documentary claims, archival photo stories.
- **Do not use when:** bright multi-colour brands; anything needing clean vector edges.
- **Common mistakes:** riso-like flat stencil overprints -> this is continuous tone per ink with only dot size; adding lines -> no line anywhere.
- **Source:** alexgreensh/anidoodle `references/styles/halftone.md` (Apache-2.0).

## Woodblock and engraving

The black-block woodcut (light only where cut, at most one colour) and the colour woodblock (keyblock plus flat colour blocks plus bokashi, ukiyo-e family) are different styles; do not mix them in one film. The copperplate engraving builds tone with burin hatching.

### Woodcut Print (`woodcut`)
- **Look:** A relief print that carves itself: each shot is one printed plate of near-black ink on laid paper inside a fixed paper margin; black is uncut wood, white is what the knife removed, so light is carved; hatching direction follows form and width follows tone; at most one colour plate, printed only in carved white. Not copperplate engraving (no swelling burin lines on white), not linocut poster art (no flat colour areas), not a thresholded photo (cuts must follow form).
- **Medium-specific moves:** characters on twos (12 fps), camera, particles, fire and light on ones (24 fps); hatching lives in each body part's local space so cuts move with limbs; only fire and liquids re-cut every 2 frames as deliberate boil; anticipation, 2-frame strike, settle plus short shake; carve reveal stroke by stroke (each 0.08-0.35 s), keyed by light (bright first, coarse to fine), radial, or downward; nothing fades in; hard cut is a new impression with two frames of misregistration; the one transition grammar is printing: brayer rolls ink, paper lays down, baren spirals, the sheet peels toward camera showing mirrored show-through; mirrored block letters read true when the sheet is pulled. Tools in 1080p px: knife 1.2-2 (contours, white halo around black figures), V-gouge 2-8 (hatching, hair), U-gouge 10-22 (rays, sky sweeps), stab radius 3-8 (snow, sparks, chips); each cut 30-160 px, blunt entry, tapered exit, chipped edge; skin carved white with features left black.
- Camera list: track with the knife tip; continuous pull-back as cuts spread; locked plate (a page of a wordless novel); slow macro push on a sculpted close-up; snap zoom and hold; frame within a frame (door, window, arch); hard cuts on the beat, each a new impression; crane up from detail to whole; rack between plates via the peel. Margin never moves; black on carved white or white on uncut black, never grey on grey; no dissolves.
- **Palette:** Two values carry everything: near-black ink and warm laid-paper white; raw wood colour only in shots showing the block; one optional colour plate reserved for the thing that matters (a flame, molten metal, a heart, a signal lamp), spreading at a peak and shrinking after, never decoration. Example plates: burnt copper-orange for heat, cold Prussian blue for water or night, deep oxblood for a wound or seal.
- **Type and captions:** Captions are letterpress in the paper margin below the plate, never over the image: an old-style letterpress serif (illustration only: IM Fell English, OFL; choose per the ice-direction skill, `references/type-pairing.md`) ~40-44 px in ink through the print texture; ink comes up in 0.1 s with no slide, fades over 0.15 s; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Titles are carved into the block (IM Fell English SC), mirrored if shown before the pull; closing text is a fresh print.
- **Sound:** Low strings (contrabass, cellos, spiccato, pizzicato), wood and skin (log drum, woodblock, frame drum), gran cassa, timpani, one tam-tam, synthesized struck metal (3-5 inharmonic partials plus hammer), bowed saw or hurdy-gurdy drone. Options: withhold the timbre of the story's object until it appears, metric modulation instead of tempo jumps, one drone under a locked plate. Foley: knife into wood (transient plus fibre tearing tied to knife speed), chips, brayer tack, paper air, baren rasp, wet peel, metals by size. True silence before the loudest moment; J/L cuts across plates. Music ducks ~8 dB and foley ducks too under voice; hard transients panned off centre; -14 LUFS; few low plain lines.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: light (light is carved: black is uncut wood and white is what the knife removed, hatching following the form with its width following tone, grazing light with a few cuts on the lit edge; two values carry everything, with at most one colour plate printed only in carved white, never grey on grey; lifts `art:tones` and `art:shading`, tone being cut density, entry Look, Palette, camera list and Common mistakes), rhythm (characters on twos while camera, particles, fire and light run on ones; a carve reveal stroke by stroke keyed by light, coarse to fine; anticipation, a 2-frame strike and a settle with a short shake; only fire and liquids re-cut as deliberate boil, entry moves), transition (printing is the one transition grammar: the brayer rolls ink, the paper lays down, the baren spirals and the sheet peels toward the camera; a hard cut is a new impression with two frames of misregistration; no dissolves, entry moves and camera list), finish (near-black ink on laid paper inside a fixed margin, each cut with a blunt entry, tapered exit and chipped edge, entry Look and moves), foley (knife into wood tied to the knife's speed, chips, brayer tack, baren rasp and a wet peel, entry Sound); serves when also: camera (a carve to follow or a plate larger than the frame: scoped: the paper margin never moves: tracks with the knife tip, a pull-back as cuts spread, a slow macro push, entry camera list), voice (a narrator: few low, plain lines, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** labour, craft and time-taken stories, social and historical narratives told as plates, wordless sequences, a single burning subject (energy, blood donation, a lit window), records being corrected or results revealed, archival portraits re-cut.
- **Do not use when:** cheerful, colourful or playful consumer tone is needed; more than one colour must carry meaning; content needs readable small UI or data; soft gradients or photoreal detail.
- **Common mistakes:** white marks on white areas are invisible -> stab cuts over dark masses; hatching every surface looks like fur or rain -> grazing light, 3-6 cuts on the lit edge only; black faces with white cuts don't read small -> skin white, features black, black keep-line around white parts; hand-drawn close-up props look like mittens -> sculpt a height field and derive cuts from its lighting; short wobbly hatch segments become chevrons -> long cuts, one broad highlight band, thin rim light, stronger halo; steam over the subject reads as flames -> draw it behind; caches shared between shots make frames worker-dependent -> each cache builds on first use; fine hatching bloats files -> higher CRF with grain tuning, clamp NaN in flat areas.
- **Variation slots:** structure: seven locked plates one per hour, or the same block recut deeper each scene (illustration only); opening: finished print on a wall then back into the block, or wood grain that becomes water or a field under the first cuts (illustration only); ending: the block cleaned to pale carved wood, or a hold on an uncarved black corner (illustration only).
- **Source:** lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar `styles/woodcut/STYLE.md` (MIT; demo media CC BY 4.0 LemoLab).

### Ukiyo-e (`ukiyoe`)
- **Look:** Every shot is a complete woodblock print on warm washi: thin double ink border, flat colour areas from separate blocks slightly out of register (1.5-2.5 px), sumi key line on top, woodgrain, baren rubbing and fine specks, hand-wiped bokashi skies, a vertical title cartouche and a red seal; depth only from stacked layers, mist bands and bold cropping. Not vector art with a paper overlay, not ink wash, not cel shading (no light and shadow on forms).
- **Medium-specific moves:** prints are mostly still, one or two small actions per view (steam, rain, a lantern, a leaf); figures and nature step at 8 fps, rain and fast water at 12 fps, camera on ones at 24 fps; printing reveal: each block lands over ~0.3 s with a diagonal baren sweep and noisy edge, sliding 5-6 px into registration; cartouches drop in (fade plus short slide); a seal stamps (scale ~1.35 to 1 in 0.2 s); hold any native move about a second or it reads as noise; key lines 2.4-3 px, variable-width tapered contours with +-20-35 % width jitter; bokashi top edge wanders +-20-30 px per column; figures small (~80-130 px at full sheet).
- Camera list: locked frame on one sheet; handscroll slide right to left through the mounting gap (shorter = urgency); very slow push toward a framing device; tilt past the border into the mount; pull-back from one sheet to many; crash-zoom into grain and fibres; pan across a three-sheet triptych; hard cut to a blank sheet as a held breath. Transitions: scroll slides, printing reveals, wash back to paper, cut to blank; no cross-dissolves or 3D flips; never rotate in 3D inside a print.
- **Palette:** A pigment set, not a wheel: 6-9 flat hues each from its own block over paper; paper is the white (foam, snow, mist, blank sky); one dominant hue family (Prussian blue) carries sky and water; red is rare (seals, a sun, one garment); far layers pale and low-contrast, near layers saturated with dark key lines. Example sets: indigo plus beni red landscape (Prussian blue, pale blue, beni, ochre, greens, sumi); interior (pale pink, mauve, grey-green, lacquer black, mica grey); autumn (persimmon, ochre, grey-green, deep brown, one blue).
- **Type and captions:** Subtitle is a horizontal cream cartouche with thick plus thin ink border and a small red seal at left, Shippori Mincho 500 in sumi, centred low; it prints in with the reveal mask and fades; covers the bottom ~150 px so keep ground lines and figures above; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). View cartouche: vertical cream slip, series tab, view title in a brush font (Yuji Syuku), seal beside it. Never floating type.
- **Sound:** Shamisen with sawari buzz, shakuhachi, koto, taiko and shime-daiko, hand-drum calls, wooden clappers, a small bell; in, yo or ritsu modes; breathy slides and pitch drops at phrase ends. Options: absolute silence as ma (cut reverb tails), one ringing buzz note, accelerating clappers, taiko heartbeat tightening to a roll, koto glissando for a season turn. Foley: baren rub (band-passed noise with slow circular modulation), scroll slide, seal thump and tacky peel, wood set down, wind chime, water, seasonal birds and insects. Calm poem-like voice; music ducked ~8 dB, ambience ~4 dB; -14 LUFS; no added grain.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: depth (scoped: depth only from stacked flat layers, mist bands and bold cropping, far layers pale and low in contrast; never a 3D rotation inside a print, entry Look, Palette and camera list), rhythm (prints mostly still with one or two small actions per view: figures and nature at 8 fps, rain and fast water at 12, the camera on ones; each block printing in over about 0.3 s with a diagonal baren sweep, any native move held about a second, entry moves), transition (scroll slides through the mounting gap, printing reveals, a wash back to paper or a cut to a blank sheet; no cross-dissolves or 3D flips, entry camera list), finish (warm washi in a thin double border, flat blocks 1.5-2.5 px out of register under a sumi key line, woodgrain, baren rubbing and fine specks, hand-wiped bokashi; no added grain, entry Look, moves and Sound), type (the subtitle as a cream cartouche with a red seal printing in with the reveal, a vertical view cartouche with a series tab and a seal; never floating type, entry Look and Type and captions), ambience (seasonal birds and insects, water and a wind chime, entry Sound); serves when also: silence (a reveal or the key line: ma, absolute silence with reverb tails cut, entry Sound), voice (a narrator: a calm, poem-like voice, entry Sound); never: light (no light and shadow on forms: flat colour blocks and bokashi only, entry Look and Do not use when).
- **Use when:** journeys and sequences of steps, one subject seen through many places or seasons, supply chains, recipes, before/after (same key block, new colours), festive panoramas, calm heritage or travel pieces.
- **Do not use when:** the content needs 3D perspective, lighting or shaded product forms; fast high-energy editing (the medium is still and held); data-dense UI or many on-screen numbers.
- **Common mistakes:** bokashi built from fill strips shows vertical seams -> build it per pixel; too much speckle or fibre looks like sandpaper -> specks alpha ~0.3, fibres ~0.09; perspective grid fields look digital -> parallel, slanted, flat layers; pine clusters as half-discs read as parasols -> flat pads with needle fans; offset curves self-intersect at bends -> build inner lines from the gentle side; breaking out of the print fails -> escape both the sheet clip and frame clip; seal multiplied on dark ground vanishes -> draw it normally there.
- **Variation slots:** structure: alternate impressions of one key block across times of day and weather, or a triptych revealed sheet by sheet (illustration only); opening: an extreme foreground detail that pulls back to find the print, or the bare inked block rubbed and lifted (illustration only); ending: print fades to paper leaving only the red seal, or the last print left as key line only (illustration only).
- **Source:** lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar `styles/ukiyoe/STYLE.md` (MIT; demo media CC BY 4.0 LemoLab).

### Ukiyo-e colour woodblock (`woodcut`, hand)
- **Look:** water-based pigment with rice paste brushed on carved cherry blocks and printed by hand with a baren on damp washi, one block per colour, registered by a corner notch and a guide.
- **Medium-specific moves:** the keyblock line is a ridge standing between two knife cuts: width steps where the carver reset the knife, edges wobble independently, every end a flat oblique chisel cut (no brush swell, no pen taper); solid blacks knife-edged; colour flat, showing plank grain and speckle where the baren rubbed thin; tone only by bokashi (a wiped gradient with a wavering edge); colour flats land a hair off their keylines, each block with its own small offset; a block is cut away where another subject owns the area, so white is bare paper. Order: keyblock first, colour blocks lightest to darkest, bokashi blocks last. Motion: each block is a pull: the baren works over the back in overlapping zigzag rows and colour comes through where it has passed; a breath between pulls; the last pull is the still.
- **Palette:** sumi `#1f1b18`, usuzumi `#c6c1b0`, yellow `#d49c3a`, greys, wood `#a77a4a`, water `#9ab5c4`, greens, Prussian blue `#2c4a78`, indigo `#3a4557` (illustration only).
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: finish (flat colour showing plank grain and baren speckle, tone only by bokashi, each block a hair off its keyline; no modelled form, so it lifts `art:shading`, entry moves), rhythm (each block a pull: the baren works in overlapping zigzag rows and colour comes through where it has passed, a breath between pulls, keyblock first, entry moves), foley (the baren rub on damp paper, the sibling `ukiyoe` Sound line; research: validate); serves when also: silence (a reveal or the key line: ma, with reverb tails cut, the sibling `ukiyoe` Sound line; research: validate).
- **Source:** alexgreensh/anidoodle `references/styles/woodcut.md` (Apache-2.0).

### Copperplate Engraving (`engraving`)
- **Look:** An intaglio plate that engraves itself: a burin cuts swelling and tapering lines into copper, a proof is pulled, and the figure builds from outline to hatching to cross-hatching; tone is made only of lines (paper is light, one line family is half-tone, a crossing family is shadow, a third lozenge family is the deepest dark); engraved lettering, magnification roundels with leaders, and transparent hand-laid washes after printing. Not a woodcut, not a loose pen sketch, not an engineering drawing, not a sepia filter.
- **Medium-specific moves:** cutting order is the animation: outline, first hatching sweeping across each form, crossing, then darks, hair, stipple; strokes at constant burin speed, many at once; a long stroke may be camera-followed, started partly cut so no frame is empty; keep a full build to a few seconds; swell law width x (0.2 + 0.8*sin(pi*u)^0.7) tapered over the first and last ~22 %; families switch on at tone thresholds ~0.14, ~0.5, ~0.75; hatch spacing ~1/180 of the figure height; one light direction (upper left), heavier outlines away from it, lines stop at occluders; roundels fly on an eased arc with a small lift, growing log-linearly; colour blooms from one point with a ragged front and wet rim that dries; nothing fades or slides like UI. Copper shots: burin held low, turned <= 15 deg off the line, swarf curling ahead with one lit edge.
- Camera list (a loupe over a sheet): loupe following the burin tip; log-zoom pull-out; push to a part and ride with its roundel; tilt/pan along the sheet at reading zoom; pull back while something flies; locked frame or <= 2 % breathing push; raking view across the copper; cut from mirrored copper to true proof. Transitions only from printmaking (proof pulled, tissue guard laid over, region burnished out and re-cut, new sheet on the bed); no dissolves or wipes.
- **Palette:** Two values, laid paper cream ~`#f1e8d2` and one warm near-black ink ~`#1c1510`; colour only as transparent wash in muted earth and mineral pigments, at most one stronger pigment, multiplied under the ink, spilling a few px past lines with tide-lines and granulation; never on lettering, rules or paper outside the figure; reserve it for the whole subject at one moment, one part, or none.
- **Type and captions:** Roman capitals (Bodoni Moda; title ~44-56 px wide-tracked, labels ~15-19 px) cut glyph by glyph; italics for scientific names (~20-24 px); script (Pinyon Script) for notes, written in one left-to-right stroke, never shrunk; nothing fades in. Subtitles: script on a deckled paper slip bottom centre, <= 2 lines, never over a label; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s); an engraved note stays chars / 12 + 1 s from when it starts writing.
- **Sound:** Foley by material: paper (peel crackle, slip tapped down, tissue rustle), press (roller rumble, thump), copper and steel (high band-passed burin hiss with chatter and faint plate ring, swarf pings, tick on lift, circular scribe, burnishing rub), ink and water (pen scratch, ticks for cut letters, water plip, wet brush swish); hatching as micro-scratches denser each pass. Quiet workroom with a faint clock. Instruments: harpsichord, string quartet, solo cello or viol, lute, chamber organ, glass harmonica; plucked and dry while cutting, sustained only when something soft arrives. Real silence. Melody an octave above the voice, music ~half under speech, -14 LUFS; a quiet curator or naturalist voice.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: light (tone made only of lines under one light from the upper left: paper is light, one hatch family the half-tone, a crossing family the shadow, a lozenge family the deepest dark, heavier outlines away from the light; lifts `art:tones` and `art:shading`, tone being line density, entry Look and moves), rhythm (the cutting order is the animation: outline, first hatching, crossing, darks, hair and stipple, strokes at constant burin speed and many at once, a full build kept to a few seconds; nothing fades or slides, entry moves), camera (a loupe over a sheet: following the burin tip, log-zoom pull-outs, a push to a part riding with its roundel, a locked frame breathing at most 2%, entry camera list), transition (only from printmaking: a proof pulled, a tissue guard laid over, a region burnished out and re-cut, a new sheet on the bed; no dissolves or wipes, entry camera list), type (roman capitals cut glyph by glyph, italics for scientific names, script notes written in one stroke, labels with leaders and magnification roundels flying on an eased arc; lifts `lint:T1` for titles cut glyph by glyph, entry Look, moves and Type and captions), finish (laid cream paper and one warm near-black ink; colour only as a transparent wash blooming from one point after printing, with tide-lines and granulation, never on lettering; at most one stronger pigment, kept for the whole subject at one moment, one part or none, entry Look, moves and Palette), foley (burin hiss with chatter, swarf pings, press rumble and thump, paper peel and tissue rustle, entry Sound), ambience (a quiet workroom with a faint clock, entry Sound); serves when also: voice (a narrator: a quiet curator or naturalist voice, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** museum labels and case screens, natural-history and scientific explainers, textbook figures with labelled parts, craft heritage and edition stories, maps and site plans with insets, certificates and finished-document moments, design revisions.
- **Do not use when:** the brand needs bold colour or energetic pace; the subject has no clear form to hatch (abstract services, UI flows); mass-market playful tone; content requires many fast cuts or dense on-screen data.
- **Common mistakes:** uniform width reads as pen -> swell hatches, pinch contours between runs, keep the minimum hair-fine; overlaps without occlusion -> subtract occluders ring by ring; outline-only parts beside a hatched body -> hatch along each part's axis with a dark at every joint; burin along the cut hides the groove -> <= 15 deg skew; regular swarf helix reads as a spring -> vary winding and twist; reflections wash out grooves -> light the copper first; small-authored roundels freeze sub-pixel -> author large, scale in; leaders hugging the subject -> add clearance; a long early stroke never draws -> look ahead by the longest stroke; push-ins cropping a fresh note -> keep consecutive details on one side; scientific names fail ASR -> give a plain spelling.
- **Variation slots:** structure: states of one plate re-cut as a place changes, or a book of small plates turned like pages with one fact each, or a dissection labelled in teaching order (illustration only); opening: a finished proof burnished back to its first line, or the title cut first with the figure growing below (illustration only); ending: plate beside its proof, or a natural-size figure held alone (illustration only).
- **Source:** lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar `styles/engraving/STYLE.md` (MIT; demo media CC BY 4.0 LemoLab).

## Screen print

### Silkscreen Travel Poster (`silkscreen-poster`)
- **Look:** A poster printed on camera: opaque, hard-edged flat inks pulled one colour at a time through a screen, 4-6 inks per poster, later ink fully covering earlier ink; skies and haze as stepped bands (flat colour, thinning stripes, next colour), never gradients; registration offsets 1-3 px leaving bare-paper crescents, a faint darker overprint sliver, ink rim light top-left and dark bottom-right, a wet sheen fading within a second, cream fibrous stock with mottle and pinholes; a dark info band at the foot. Not risograph (translucent, halftone), not ukiyo-e (key-block line, bokashi), not woodcut, not low-poly.
- **Medium-specific moves:** pulls are linear and steady (a squeegee does not ease) behind a ragged squeegee front with blade, ink bead and a sliver of handle; accent inks start half a beat after their layer's main ink; info items print left to right on beat subdivisions; there is no fade anywhere; screen lift hinges up with squash and growing ring shadow in ~0.4 s; palette re-ink: a wide squeegee crosses diagonally in ~0.35 s, geometry identical, only inks change; split-fountain pulls put several colours in one bead; loose multi-plane layers parallax with paper gaps and thickness shadows, then snap into register on one hit; texture budget kept light (mottle ~55 %, pinholes ~75 %).
- Camera list: truck with the squeegee, slight roll, frame edge in view; locked top-down full sheet; fast pull-back as the screen lifts; push and tilt to the info band; truck through a foreground silhouette into the next poster; crane through loose planes; slow lateral dolly along a row of prints; push into the mesh weave; log-zoom pull-back to the poster on a wall (hold >= 3 s). Close-ups keep the sheet edge in frame; layers change by squeegee, posters through foreground silhouettes or a table move; no dissolves, no bare cuts.
- **Palette:** One palette per poster, <= 6 inks by role: light sky, deep sky/bands, far, mid, near (darkest, also the info band), one bright accent (sun, route, key number), optional lit-face ink; values step far light to near dark (depth is value, never blur); the accent is rare; paper is the brightest colour; a series is one template re-inked per item; text ink chosen by contrast against the ink beneath. Sets (sky1 to near, accent): dawn `#F6CD98 #EE9A73 #9A7FA8 #3E7079 #1E3B3F #FBE6B4`; harbour noon `#E8F0F2 #7FB3C8 #D9C7A3 #2F6E8E #173247 #E8553D`; winter `#EEF1F4 #B8C9D9 #8B9BB0 #4A5D78 #1F2A3C #F2B134`.
- **Type and captions:** Condensed display for names and values (Big Shoulders Display, League Gothic), auto-fit; geometric sans for letter-spaced labels (Outfit 600); headers printed into the sky, tracked out; info band printed last. The poster text is the subtitle; with a voice, subtitles are a printed band pull, never a floating caption. Each text holds >= chars/12 + 1 s (min 1.5 s) after its last item lands and >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s) with voice, rounded up to the beat; on a wall shot labels >= ~18 px, values >= ~30 px at 1080p.
- **Sound:** Acoustic hand-played instruments chosen from the place (strummed and slide guitar, harmonica, upright bass, cajon or brushes, banjo, fiddle, accordion, harmonium, ukulele; folk, surf, bossa or brass band). One pull, one hit; registration snap is a loud downbeat; accelerate by subdividing. Foley: squeegee on mesh (band-passed noise with ~220-280 Hz mesh grain, faster = brighter), wet ink squelch, screen clack and hinge creak, paper stamps, ticks for dashed routes, clamp, drying-rack clips; finished scenes may wake up with their ambience via J/L cuts. Music drops out a beat before key facts. Music ducks a few dB under foley, -14 LUFS.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: rhythm (pulls are linear and steady behind a ragged squeegee front, accent inks half a beat after their layer's main ink, info items printing left to right on beat subdivisions, no fade anywhere; one pull, one hit, the registration snap a loud downbeat, entry moves and Sound), finish (opaque hard-edged inks, each later one fully covering the earlier, skies as stepped bands, registration offsets of 1-3 px leaving bare-paper crescents, an ink rim light top-left and dark bottom-right, a wet sheen fading within a second, cream fibrous stock with a light texture budget, entry Look and moves), depth (scoped: loose layers parallax with paper gaps and thickness shadows, then snap into register on one hit; values step far light to near dark, never blur, entry moves and Palette), type (the poster text is the subtitle: condensed display names and values, letter-spaced labels, headers printed into the sky, a dark info band at the foot printed last, entry Look and Type and captions), transition (layers change by squeegee, posters through foreground silhouettes or a table move; no dissolves and no bare cuts, entry camera list); serves when also: camera (a sheet, a print run or a wall of posters: scoped: close-ups keep the sheet edge in frame; trucking with the squeegee, a locked top-down sheet, a pull-back to the poster on a wall held at least 3 s, entry camera list), silence (a key fact: the music drops out a beat before it, entry Sound), ambience (a finished scene that wakes up: its ambience through a J or L cut, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** destination and travel guides, trail or attraction guides, event and festival posters, product drops with colourways, seasonal hours notices, route and delivery-path stories, a series of places or variants ending on a wall.
- **Do not use when:** photographic realism or soft gradients are required; the content is long narration with little factual payload; dense data tables or UI; a dark, solemn register where poster cheer misfits.
- **Common mistakes:** stroke edge effects draw internal lines on unioned paths -> fills only; a full-rectangle screen shadow muddies the print -> ring shadow; cool shadow facets beside water read as water -> shadows in the near ink; jagged ink beads read as paper -> round bead; parallax alone reads as moving mountains -> paper gaps and thickness while loose, snapped with a sound; silhouette wipes hold a dark blank -> centre the switch so full cover lasts ~1 frame; very wide banners are slivers -> about 4:1; random coloured table dots read as confetti -> a real workbench; too many items at honest reading times overrun -> print items side by side or leave secondary ones for the final wall.
- **Variation slots:** structure: one poster re-inked for several nights with the band changing, or a multi-plane street walk printing each sign as it passes, or a misprint drifting until the last pull lands true (illustration only); opening: light through a blank mesh as a stencil is exposed, or the accent ink alone on bare paper (illustration only); ending: prints swaying on a drying rack, or the squeegee runs dry leaving a white silhouette (illustration only).
- **Source:** lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar `styles/silkscreen-poster/STYLE.md` (MIT; demo media CC BY 4.0 LemoLab).

## Cut paper, collage and pop-up

Scoped difference: plain cut-paper collage has no paint and no gap between layers; toy-theatre paper craft has painted and printed papers in a stage with real gaps; pop-up books spring sets open; red paper-cut is one colour with light through the holes; photo collage cuts real photographs with a white scissor edge.

### Cut-paper collage (`fox`, hand)
- **Look:** zero drawn lines: torn pieces with a white fibrous edge, scissor pieces crisp, each piece lifting a soft shadow off the piece below.
- **Medium-specific moves:** built bottom of the stack up: frame 0 is the bare backing board; passes (sky and bands, terrain tier by tier, subject from the farthest piece to the eye glint, foreground details) with a beat of rest between; each piece travels held above the stack (shadow wide, far, soft), is lowered and pressed, and the shadow pulls in tight; big full-bleed sheets slide in fast from their own edge, small pieces are fiddly and slow; the held last second is the hero still exactly.
- **Palette:** coloured papers chosen for the subject; white fibre edges as the only line.
- **Sound:** paper tear, scissor snip, press thump; light acoustic or playful percussion.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: composite (each piece lifts a soft shadow off the piece below: held above the stack with a wide, far, soft shadow, lowered and pressed until the shadow pulls in tight, entry Look and moves), rhythm (built from the bottom of the stack up, frame 0 the bare backing board, passes from sky to the eye glint with a beat of rest between; big sheets slide in fast from their own edge, small pieces are fiddly and slow; the held last second is the hero still, entry moves), finish (zero drawn lines: torn pieces with a white fibrous edge and crisp scissor pieces, entry Look and Palette); serves when also: depth (near and far, or stacked pieces: scoped: no gap between layers, depth only from the lift shadow, section intro and entry Look).
- **Use when:** warm brands, children, storytelling, nature, posters.
- **Do not use when:** precision technical content; photographic products.
- **Common mistakes:** painted edges and dry brush (that is mid-century gouache) -> torn fibres and lift shadows; pieces fading in -> pieces are placed and pressed.
- **Source:** alexgreensh/anidoodle `references/styles/foxDraw.md`, `references/styles.md` (Apache-2.0).

### Toy-theatre paper collage (`paperCraft`, hand)
- **Look:** a cardboard toy theatre on a kraft table: coloured and printed papers (cut or torn), gouache cut-outs with a white sticker margin, every piece with a card edge and a real gap to the flat behind.
- **Medium-specific moves:** gouache brush strokes on cut-outs with a painted shade side; printed motifs on papers; edges: scissor-crisp or torn, then a darker card edge, then the white margin (never following a concave detail too closely); each flat throws a soft shadow onto the flat behind sized by the gap; flats slide in behind the theatre walls. Order: table, proscenium and floor set down, scene back to front (sky drop flown in, sun lowered on its stick, pieces pushed up, slid in, stood up), curtains last. Stop-motion on twos: a piece travels held above the stack, lands, jiggles a few degrees and a pixel or two (damped) and is still; ink and paint never move.
- **Palette:** kraft `#b8905f`, theatre teal `#2f6f73` with gold `#e1b54a`, theatre red `#b8302c`, sea blues, gouache pastels (illustration only).
- **Sound:** card slides, paper, small theatre ambience; music-box or folk.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: depth (scoped: a cardboard toy theatre with a real gap from every piece to the flat behind, flats sliding in behind the theatre walls; never faked 3D, entry Look, moves and Common mistakes), composite (each flat throws a soft shadow onto the flat behind sized by the gap, entry moves), light (a painted shade side on each gouache cut-out, entry moves), finish (coloured and printed papers, scissor-crisp or torn edges, a darker card edge and a white sticker margin, on a kraft table, entry Look and moves), rhythm (table, proscenium and floor set down, the scene back to front, curtains last; stop-motion on twos, each piece landing with a damped jiggle of a few degrees, entry moves), ambience (a small theatre ambience, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** children's stories, theatre and culture, gift brands, whimsical explainers.
- **Do not use when:** technical precision; photoreal products.
- **Common mistakes:** no gaps between layers (that is plain cut paper) -> real space and gap shadows; smooth tweens -> on twos with a damped jiggle.
- **Source:** alexgreensh/anidoodle `references/styles/paperCraft.md` (Apache-2.0).

### Paper Pop-up Book (`paper-popup`)
- **Look:** A modelled hardcover pop-up book (cloth cover with foil title, page block, curling leaf) on a photoreal desk under HDRI room light and a real macro lens becomes a stage: one board stands up as the backdrop, the lower page is the ground, every set piece is a flat cut-paper card that pops up around its bottom edge, and actors are 3-5 cm flat cut-paper cartoons with a thick off-white border, grey cardboard edge and grain, casting cut-out-shaped shadows and going soft in real lens blur. NOT a papercut silhouette film (no one-colour lace, no backlight), not a 3D cartoon (nothing modelled round), not claymation (paper hinges, folds, curls and slides, never squishes).
- **Medium-specific moves:** real metric units (page 0.3-0.5 m, actors 3-5 cm, near plane a few mm) so HDRI, shadows and DOF behave like macro; cut-paper finish: dark-brown ink outline (never black), silhouette dilated into a grey cardboard edge offset slightly down plus a wide off-white border, shared paper grain on top; shading = one hard crescent, wobbly low-harmonic shapes, simple faces; pop-up cards have art on the front and plain paper on the back with alpha-tested coverage and an alpha-mapped depth material so the shadow is the cut-out silhouette; anything "3D" is several flat cards; neutral tone mapping; hand-built DOF with a spiral gather that keeps far samples off a sharp foreground, then light bloom, vignette, warmth; on ones (60 fps), all a pure function of time; paper moves stiffly, life from easing (back overshoot for pop-ups, springs for things on strings, squash and stretch on characters); pop-ups rise back to front (delay grows with distance from the reader), far pieces lie back, near ones fold forward face-down, folding down in the same order; plants sway a few hundredths of a radian; characters redrawn per quantized pose (walk phase from distance walked so feet don't slide, arms, eyes, mouth, look, blink); turning around = a horizontal mirror flip between front and back drawings, never a Y rotation; jumps are parabolic arcs with squash at both ends and a paper puff; page turn = many-segment strip curling along its length; the room never moves; transitions: page turns, the book closing and reopening, hard cuts on a paper sound; never a video dissolve inside the book.
  - Camera: one macro camera keyed as position/target/fov/aperture/focus with monotone-cubic interpolation, a new key list = a hard cut; eye-level macro track; pull up to 3/4 view of the spread; descending dolly from human height; push-in on a face; reverse shot from inside the book to the blurred room; rack focus paper to real object; top-down over the open spread; locked frame on a pull-tab; rising crane leaving the book small. Actors in focus, room objects as soft foreground or background, near pop-ups kept out of the lens path.
- **Palette:** saturated children's-book colour, never neon; the real world stays natural and slightly warm so paper pops; one dominant hue family per spread so a page turn is also a colour change; a few recurring accents (a red, a yellow, a gold) stitch the book together; ink dark brown, border off-white, card backs plain paper; unfinished or "outside the story" pages lose colour (graphite on cream); night is a change of paper art while the real lamp stays warm. Examples: green meadow under sky blue; pink-and-lilac market with mint accents; ochre desert with a turquoise oasis.
- **Type and captions:** rounded sans for subtitles, bubbles and signs (e.g. Fredoka); chunky display face for cover, banners and action words (e.g. Lilita One); storybook serif for taglines (e.g. IM Fell English); a playful CJK face (e.g. ZCOOL KuaiLe) with the Latin face in its stack, subset and re-subset after every text change. Narration subtitles bottom centre ~40 px, cream with thick dark-brown outline and soft shadow, second language smaller below; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Speech bubbles live in the paper world (white card, ink outline, tail projected to the speaker every frame, pop with overshoot, type on with blips). Titles are paper objects (a cardboard sign dropping on strings and swinging, the cover as film title); italic gradient action words with double stroke and rays used sparingly, like a game rating.
- **Sound:** paper foley is the signature: page swish, cardboard thump, pop-up snap (short sine sweep + crinkle), fold and crease, pull-tab slide, tiny tap footsteps with crinkle, slat flaps, string-drop tinks, spine creak and closing thump, all synthesizable from the page's event list; character voices as typing blips (one waveform and pitch range per character, a blip every other letter); one warm storyteller narrator in picture-book sentences; whimsical acoustic or toy music (music box, pizzicato strings, clarinet, glockenspiel, ukulele, toy piano, brushes), options: warmer cue for the real room, bar-aligned edit so the ending lands on a picture event, pull-tab clicks setting a cue's pulse. Ambience: room tone and a clock for the room, one bed per spread. Silence = book held still with room tone only, before a turn that matters. Voice high-passed and compressed above the music, smooth duck release, limit SFX per channel; -14 LUFS.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: depth (scoped: a modelled book on a photoreal desk in real metric units under a real macro lens; every set piece a flat cut-paper card popping up around its bottom edge, anything 3D built from several flat cards, entry Look and moves), light (HDRI room light with real macro depth of field, one hard crescent of shading on each cut-out, entry Look and moves), composite (every card casting a shadow in its own cut-out shape, actors going soft in real lens blur, entry Look and Common mistakes), finish (a dark-brown ink outline, never black, a grey cardboard edge and a wide off-white border with shared paper grain; neutral tone mapping, light bloom, vignette and warmth; lifts `slop:N11` for that bloom and vignette, entry moves), rhythm (on ones as a pure function of time: paper moves stiffly with life from easing, pop-ups rising back to front, characters redrawn per quantized pose with feet that never slide, a turn as a mirror flip, entry moves), camera (one macro camera: eye-level tracks, pull-ups to the spread, a reverse shot from inside the book, rack focus from paper to a real object, a rising crane leaving the book small, entry camera list), transition (page turns, the book closing and reopening, hard cuts on a paper sound; never a video dissolve inside the book, entry moves), ambience (room tone and a clock for the room, one bed per spread, entry Sound); serves when also: type (words on screen: speech bubbles in the paper world with a tail to the speaker, typed on with blips, and titles as paper objects; lifts `lint:T1` and `slop:N9` for the bubbles, entry Type and captions), voice (a narrator: one warm storyteller in picture-book sentences, characters as typing blips, entry Sound), silence (a turn that matters: the book held still with room tone only, entry Sound).

## Shadow puppetry (merged: two sources)

Translucent carved and dyed hide puppets on a lamp-lit screen, rods below, jointed limbs, stepped motion (about 10 fps). Both sources agree: rods go down and outward, never across another figure; keep overlaps (which multiply darker) off focal points.

### Shadow Puppetry (`shadow-puppet`)
- **Look:** Flat jointed puppets cut from translucent dyed hide, carved like lace, pressed on a white cotton screen and lit from behind by flickering oil lamps; dyes glow by transmitted light, carved holes shine brightest, overlaps go darker, thin rods show as soft grey lines. Not a black silhouette film, not front-lit paper-cut, not stained glass (no lead lines), not flat vector cut-out.
- **Medium-specific moves:** 24 fps continuous for puppets and camera, only swapped effect pieces (fire, water) step at 12 fps; hand tremor on every puppet (a few px, ~0.02 rad, stronger under tension); legs, plumes, tassels simulated as damped pendulums; operatic phrasing: shuffle run, hop, crouch, freeze pose ~1 s on a percussion accent, head turn; faces never change, attitude is head angle plus body tilt; press-on arrivals come large and blurred and snap sharp (~0.5 s), exits grow, blur and fade (~1 s); a new lamp flares with overshoot, a snuffed lamp gutters; lamp count is the brightness dial (exposure falls as 1/sqrt(lamps lit)); fire, smoke and water are carved pieces on rods, never particles; cloth and frame never move, nothing morphs.
- Camera list: audience-seat wide with heads below; push through the frame onto the cloth; lateral pan across a wide cloth; follow a puppet with slight zoom; crash zoom of a few frames on a percussion hit; whip pan along a moving piece; locked wide for counted stages; close on a carved detail; tilt ground strip to top; truck round to the mirrored backstage view; pull back into the dark room. Transitions: lamps dimming, blurred foreground pillar wipe, a piece lifting off in light, flash to warm white; never a generic crossfade.
- **Palette:** All colour is transmitted and warm, nothing brighter than the lamp; 3-5 saturated dyes plus raw hide (neutral) plus ink (outlines, hair, boots); holes are the brightest value; night is fewer lamps, never added blue; thin things that must read (strings, text) are dark. Sets: court (vermilion, ochre, malachite), river (indigo, jade, one ochre), harvest (flame orange, gold, one green). Warm vignette toward brown, never grey.
- **Type and captions:** Storyteller's placard (narrow dark-lacquer board, thin gold line, small carved seal) or a label on a rod; classical OFL serif (Cormorant Garamond 600), 40-50 px, cream on dark, short slide in; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Titles are puppets: dark hide plaques with letters cut out so words glow, carried on rods, pressed on and lifted off.
- **Sound:** Percussion first, not piano and strings: low barrel drum, hard woodblock clapper, cymbals, small gong with rising pitch, big gong with falling pitch, bamboo clapper, pellet drum, three-string pluck, bamboo flute for quiet; a bright nasal two-string fiddle (pentatonic or a sorrowful microtonal mode with raised 4th, lowered 7th); a shrill reed at most once. Foley: wooden clapper, match strike, lamp fwoomp and pfft with sizzle, rod taps on cloth, dry leather flaps, hide and wood creaks, cloth rustle, backstage room tone. Silence hard-cuts the whole bed with at most one sound inside; stops <= 30 ms. Storyteller voice in small-theatre reverb; music ducked ~12 dB, foley ~4 dB, voice >= 12 dB above bed; -14 LUFS.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: light (flickering oil lamps behind a white cotton screen: dyes glow by transmitted light, carved holes shine brightest, overlaps go darker; lamp count is the brightness dial, a new lamp flaring and a snuffed one guttering; nothing brighter than the lamp, night is fewer lamps, never added blue, entry Look, moves and Palette), composite (puppets pressed on the screen, arriving large and blurred and snapping sharp, rods as soft grey lines running down and outward, entry Look, moves and merged section), rhythm (puppets and camera continuous at 24 fps with only swapped effect pieces stepping at 12; hand tremor on every puppet, legs and tassels as damped pendulums, operatic phrasing with a freeze pose of about 1 s on a percussion accent, entry moves), transition (lamps dimming, a blurred foreground pillar wipe, a piece lifting off in light or a flash to warm white; never a generic crossfade, entry camera list), finish (translucent dyed hide carved like lace with dense openwork, a warm vignette toward brown, never grey; lifts `slop:N11` for that vignette, entry Look, Palette and Common mistakes), music (percussion first: barrel drum, clapper, cymbals and gongs that the poses freeze on, a two-string fiddle and a three-string pluck; never piano and strings, entry moves and Sound); serves when also: voice (a narrator: a storyteller voice in small-theatre reverb at least 12 dB over the bed, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** fables, legends, lessons, a framed memory, "how it is really made" or "who pulls the strings" stories, processes with stages or states (seasons, machine states), layered systems shown as x-ray.
- **Do not use when:** the piece needs photoreal product detail or UI screens; the brand needs cool or neutral colour (everything leans warm); content needs fast data readouts or dense text; a modern corporate tone without a theatre frame.
- **Common mistakes:** flat clip-art look -> hide texture, denser openwork, deeper dyes, carved outlines everywhere; figures unreadable in profile -> bigger head, fuller lower body, collar piece, contrasting sleeves; radiating flames read as saw blades or petals -> fat-based tongues with hooked tips and alternating curl; grey overexposed corners -> warm vignette; thin pale pieces vanish -> make them dark; smoke invisible on bright cloth -> dark wisps, warm additive only near a lamp; push-in on an edge lamp shoves figures out -> push on frame centre; beds leaking into silence -> hard-cut them.
- **Variation slots:** structure: a procession pan where each figure adds a dye, or rival hands arguing through puppets with backstage cutaways (illustration only); opening: backstage first, or one carved hole of light widening on a dark screen (illustration only); ending: the lamp carried away so the cloth darkens from one side, or puppets lifted off one by one (illustration only).

### Chinese shadow puppetry (pi ying) (`34_shadowpuppet`, implemented, quality 3 stars)
- **Look:** translucent dyed leather pieces with carved openwork patterns, riveted joints, rods to the bottom edge, warm oil-lamp glow behind a cloth screen, overlaps darkening where light passes two layers, stiff jointed motion stepped at 10fps.
- **Medium-specific moves:** piece = fill colour, carve patterns out inside the clip, stroke a same-colour "leather rim" of 2 x edge so carving never breaks the outline, then a 2.6px dark brown `#3a160a` edge. Carving library: fish-roe dots (s 14-22, r 3-3.5), 5-petal flowers (s 36-46), cloud spirals (line 3.4, s 24-40), lattice (s 26-32), incised lines (3-7px). Stamp each group onto the screen: multiply a soft offset copy (alpha 0.28, blur 6, offset (7,5)) for a not-quite-flat puppet, then multiply the piece (transmitted light = screen x leather, overlaps darken naturally). White areas become fully carved-out openings leaving only the rim (rule for all silhouette styles). Opera face: carve out the whole face, outline only, then phoenix eyes, willow brows, dotted lips. Screen: radial `#fff6d6 -> #f2d7a0 -> #cf9a58 -> #7a4a20`, flame flicker 0.5 noise(3ts) + 0.3 noise(9ts) with ts stepped at 15fps, light centre drifting +/-26/+/-18px, hotspot radius 260+40 flicker, 3px cloth weave cached. Motion: whole figures sway +/-0.012 rad, heads +/-0.06 at the neck rivet, actions stepped at 10fps, loose wrists dangle 0.35 sin(11 ls)(1-0.6 action); tails as 4 hinged segments each 0.3 sin(7ts - 0.9i) + 0.38 for whip lag; rods 5px dark brown blurred 1.2px multiplied, running from joints to the bottom. Motif loops: lamp flicker across the screen, a butterfly flipping wings via scaleX at 12fps on its rod, dangling hair ornaments. Signature transition: lamp lit behind the screen (old frame multiplied rgba(70,34,12, min(1, 2.2p)) as if the light dies; new frame revealed by a radial mask solid to 0.55R, R = 1500 inOut, flame jitter +/-7%; additive warm glow sin(pi p) with a small flame).
- **Palette:** red `#c0352a`, green `#2f7d4c`, amber `#d99a2e`, yellow `#e2bd4a`, blue `#2c5a8a`, orange `#d9782a`; screen `#fff6d6 -> #7a4a20`; edge `#3a160a`.
- **Sound:** not specified in source; score in instruments native to the period or medium.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: light (a warm oil-lamp glow behind the cloth with flicker, transmitted through dyed leather so overlaps go darker, entry Look and moves), composite (each piece multiplied with a soft offset shadow, rods multiplied down to the bottom edge, entry moves), rhythm (stiff jointed motion stepped at 10 fps, sway at the rivets, loose parts dangling, entry moves), transition (the lamp lit behind the screen, entry signature transition), foley (rod taps, leather and lamp sounds, the sibling `shadow-puppet` Sound line; research: validate); serves when also: silence (a reveal or the key line: a hard-cut silence with at most one sound inside, the sibling `shadow-puppet` Sound line; research: validate).
- **Use when:** Chinese folk tales and festivals, heritage education, storytelling for children, theatrical brand films.
- **Do not use when:** realism; fast fluid motion; budget under about 121ms per frame (each piece its own layer).
- **Common mistakes:** red over green overlap multiplied to a black trapezoid at the focal point -> keep overlaps off focal areas; a solid colour leg block covered the white chest -> carve whites out; rods crossing other figures look messy -> route rods down and outward.
- **Known weakness:** overlaps go black; 121ms per frame is slow.

## Glass and thread

### Stained Glass (`stained-glass`)
- **Look:** A medieval stained-glass window seen from inside a dark stone hall: cut pieces of textured coloured glass in near-black lead cames, faces and folds painted in brown grisaille; glass glows only when the sun is behind it and throws a soft coloured copy onto large irregular flagstones; unlit panes are dark and milky; secular subjects only (no crosses, saints, halos or religious imagery). Not vector art with black outlines, not a kaleidoscope or Voronoi filter, not Art Nouveau lamp glass, not mosaic (mosaic reflects, glass transmits).
- **Medium-specific moves:** figures step at 8 fps, light, camera, shafts and dust on ones (24 fps); rigid pieces on 2D forward kinematics, long bodies as spine chains; freeze rule: a pane's figures stop when the light leaves; things appear by being lit, never faded in; glass never bends or morphs, expressions swap face pieces; crack: jagged lines race from impact in ~1/4 s, bright with light, short white flash and 2-frame shake; re-leading: piece groups slide one beat each with a small outward bump along the lead, each ending in a weld spark, cracks becoming mending leads; lit panes ~2-3x backlight with a soft skewed moving band edge; hue-preserving tone mapping so blue stays jewel blue while a sun disc burns white; bright glass eats into the lead via bloom; floor projection blurred, ~20 % desaturated, stone-modulated, haloed; shafts additive with dust, near 0 at the window, peaking ~40 % down; piece texture with +-10 % hue drift and +-9 % thickness.
- Camera list: truck sideways with the band of light; locked close-up on one pane; pull back to the whole window; tilt down the shafts to the floor then top-down; hold through a crack with no cut; slow push toward a small light; tilt up to a rose or tracery; rack between a lit and a dark pane; low angle along the embrasure; return to an earlier framing under new light. Subjects big (close-ups ~1.3-2.3x a lancet width), revealed things near centre >= ~1/8 frame width; transitions are moving light, shafts, a crack, darkness; never a dissolve, wipe or flip.
- **Palette:** Two hues carry the picture (one deep, one hot), three or four more only as small touches (e.g. cobalt ground, ruby, touches of gold, green, murrey); greenish white glass sparingly; flesh darker than expected or backlit faces burn white; a story light may take a colour no glass uses; the sun's colour is the clock (blue-white dawn, near-white noon, gold afternoon, deep orange dusk via intensity rather than redness, weak blue moonlight); stone and lead take colour only from spilled light.
- **Type and captions:** Subtitles on a parchment banderole near the bottom (curled ends, gentle sag, thin rule), an old-style serif ~44 px dark ink (font names in this entry illustration only; choose per the ice-direction skill, `references/type-pairing.md`; Cinzel is a default-look face that needs a D-entry reason), unfurling from the centre, text fading in only once >= 75 % open, padded well past the text; hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Titles are incised Roman capitals (Cinzel 600) in the stone with gilding, revealed by a moving band of light, small lines >= 30 px; in the glass, text only as grisaille on a white-glass scroll.
- **Sound:** Organ (pedal drone, soft stops, full), harp, hand chimes, tubular bells, recorder or chant-like voice, water-glass notes, timpani, hurdy-gurdy drone, psaltery, portative organ; church modes, open fifths, drones, parallel organum; no generic piano or strings. Options: unmetred chant for darkness, one "light" note per waking pane, a pedal that holds then cuts, a rising scale one step per lit piece. Foley: struck glass with inharmonic partials ~1 : 2.32 : 4.25 : 6.63 : 9.38, crack as snap plus thump plus tinkle rain, lead stick-slip creak, gritty glass-in-came slide, solder hiss and tick, stone-hall ambience, birds in the vault, cooling iron ticks. Silence is a hard stop including tails. Big reverberant mix, heavy hits off word onsets, music ducked ~9 dB, -14 LUFS, gentle even storyteller.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: light (glass glows only when the sun is behind it and throws a soft coloured copy onto the flagstones; unlit panes dark and milky; lit panes with a soft moving band edge, hue-preserving tone mapping, shafts with dust; the sun's colour is the clock, entry Look, moves and Palette), depth (scoped: the panes stay flat and never bend; space comes only from the dark stone hall around the window, the shafts, the projected copy on the flagstones and low angles along the embrasure, entry Look, moves and camera list), rhythm (figures step at 8 fps while light, camera, shafts and dust run on ones; a pane's figures stop when the light leaves; things appear by being lit, never faded in, entry moves), transition (moving light, shafts, a crack or darkness; never a dissolve, wipe or flip, entry camera list), finish (textured coloured glass with small hue and thickness drift in near-black lead cames, faces and folds in brown grisaille, bright glass eating into the lead through bloom; lifts `slop:N11` for that bloom, entry Look and moves), ambience (a stone-hall ambience, birds in the vault, cooling iron ticks, entry Sound); serves when also: voice (a narrator: a gentle, even storyteller, entry Sound).
- **Use when:** stories told as chapters or steps over a day, seasons or shifts, reinvention, injury and recovery, restoration, night-vigil portraits, product lineups or cycles drawn as lancets, roses and borders, heritage and institutional history.
- **Do not use when:** any religious reading would be inappropriate or the subject itself is religious iconography (the style is secular by rule); fast-paced energetic content; data-heavy UI; bright modern minimal brands.
- **Common mistakes:** hidden leads show through front pieces -> erase the surface under each piece before stroking its lead; pastel glass -> hue-preserving tone mapping and darker flesh; red dusk over blue glass makes mud -> keep blue alive, raise intensity; Voronoi reads as a filter and flat fills as clip-art -> hand-cut lozenge quarries, texture on every piece, heavy grisaille; a sharp floor patch looks like a decal -> blur, desaturate, stone-modulate, halo; full-strength shafts at the window wash out the pane -> fade them near it; top-down projection is flipped -> put the wall at the frame bottom; patch-map canvas size mismatch draws into a corner -> one constant size; carved text in a dark wide is unreadable -> push in and park a light on it.
- **Variation slots:** structure: a rose window lit petal by petal like hours, or one tall lancet read bottom to top, or two facing windows lit morning and evening (illustration only); opening: only a coloured patch on the floor then tilt up to its source, or a single piece cut and leaded on the bench (illustration only); ending: clouds cover the sun and every pane greys at once, or a new pane is leaded into an empty lancet (illustration only).

### Hand embroidery (`embroidery`, hand)
- **Look:** stranded cotton floss (two or three strands, one gold accent thread) stitched through natural plain-weave linen drum-tight in a wooden hoop.
- **Medium-specific moves:** stem stitch for stems, split stitch for edges, satin across a form following it (spans past about 9 mm split at the axis), fishbone for leaves, long-and-short for blended colour, French knots for centres and pollen, running stitch for a flight line; no drawn outline: edges are the pinched ends of stitches. Each stitch has a lit and a shade side, twist grooves with a glint, a hair of cast shadow; sheen depends on the stitch angle to the light, so direction reads as form. Linen computed per pixel (warp over weft, slubs, pin holes), slack cloth outside the hoop, the hoop casting its shadow. Motion: stitches made one by one, each growing from hole to hole with the needle at its head and thread trailing; knots show their wraps before pulling down.
- **Palette:** oatmeal linen, sage, olive, moss, white, shell, butter, ochre, cornflower, lavenders, coral to oxblood, gold, silver.
- **Sound:** needle through cloth, thread pull, hoop creak; folk acoustic.
- **Craft profile:** family default; needs also: light (each stitch with a lit and a shade side and a glint on its twist grooves; sheen follows the stitch angle to the light, so direction reads as form, entry moves), composite (a hair of cast shadow under each stitch, the hoop casting its shadow on the cloth, entry moves), finish (drum-tight linen computed per pixel, warp over weft with slubs and pin holes, slack cloth outside the hoop; edges are the pinched ends of stitches, never a drawn outline, entry Look, moves and Common mistakes), rhythm (stitches made one by one, each growing from hole to hole with the needle at its head and the thread trailing; knots show their wraps before pulling down, entry moves).
- **Use when:** craft brands, heritage, gifts, slow living, botanical subjects, handmade claims.
- **Do not use when:** speed, technology, data.
- **Common mistakes:** flat fills (that is cut paper) -> every mark is a thread line with direction; drawn outlines -> outlines are thread too.
- **Source:** alexgreensh/anidoodle `references/styles/embroidery.md` (Apache-2.0).
