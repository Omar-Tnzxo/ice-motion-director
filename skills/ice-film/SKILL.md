# ice-film

Workflow for motion films made from scratch, from the brief to the delivered film on the ten-stage spine. It never restates a domain rule; it points to the skill that holds it.

## 1. First step

Look for `ice/state.json` in the working folder.
- Missing: stop reading this skill. Open the router (`ice-motion-director/SKILL.md`) and follow it; it creates the project, runs the stage 01 intake and calls this workflow back.
- Present: read it and resume at `current` in the recorded `mode`. Never re-ask what the files answer.

Wrong route, now or mid-run (a teaching voiceover, footage to cut, a reference to remake, ideas only, Lottie or UI motion; a logo sting rendered as video stays here): go back through the router and record why in `history`.

## 2. Inputs and start

1. Read `ice/BRIEF.md` and `ice/DIRECTION.md`. When `ice/DIRECTION.md` is missing, create it with the header of `ice-direction/references/decision-process.md` section 7; never hand back for it.
2. Read the truth sheet `ice/assets/TRUTH.md` (ice-assets). No claim, number, logo, face or screen enters the film unless it is there or marked illustrative.
3. Size the job (depth table below) and pick the crew row (`references/film-setup.md` section 2) before dispatching any sub-agent. A short prompt gets the same floor as a detailed one: fill gaps from the type file and ice-direction, log each decision, never fall back to a template.

**Read budget.** Each `## Spine stages` row marks its files **Required** (open them, in the order listed) or **Only if <condition>** (open only when the condition holds). A file named with a section: read only that section. Open nothing else, and nothing a file merely mentions, unless the stage cannot be finished without it. `references/` and `roles/` paths are inside this skill; a path starting with another skill's name is in that skill's folder, next to this one.

**Stops and approvals.** The run waits for the user only at the router's stops table (`ice-motion-director/SKILL.md` section 5, "Stops"). Every other "ask" in this skill and its files means, in auto mode, decide, log it in `ice/DIRECTION.md` and report it in the delivery note; in step mode, put it in that stage's review card. Approval: the user in step mode; in auto mode the stage critic the crew row assigns (`references/film-setup.md` section 2), then the approver role (`ice-direction/references/roles.md` section 5), who checks the row's "Done when" item by item. **Clarity reads** 1 to 3 in the rows below are the cold-viewer gate (`ice-qa/references/clarity-gate.md`): a FAIL blocks 07 or 10 in every mode.

## Spine stages

The router owns the spine and the `stages` map in `ice/state.json`; this section says what each stage means for a film made from scratch. Stage numbers are always two digits; steps inside a stage (truth pass, openings step, cue sheet step) carry names. Each type file has one machine-readable line `Stage order: <stages>`, copied into `order`.

**Parallel lanes** and the machine-readable `Stage order:` form: `ice-motion-director/SKILL.md` section 4 (`01 02 03 [04 05] 06 [07 08a] 08b 09 10` by default; a bracket is a lane). Real-screens-led films (app ad, UI walkthrough, product launch with screens) capture the real screens in 01 and run 05 before 04, because the look is the captured UI.

**Depth by size.** Sized at 01; the size changes the paperwork, never the truth pass, the stills, the richness floor or the critic.

| Size | Film jobs | How the spine runs here |
|---|---|---|
| Micro: 6 s or less, or one asset | logo sting, one stat, effect loop, quote card | Three passes: 01, 02 and 05 condensed (the micro count of style frames in `references/storyboard.md` section 7), with 03, 04 and 06 `not_used` and folded into one plan in `ice/plan/structure.md` (structure, script, beats, cues); then 07 and 08; then 09 (the micro clarity read, one critic round plus a repair pass) and 10. Lean crew |
| Standard: 7 to 60 s, its cutdowns and versions included | app ad, social short, hype promo, launch, industry spot, short brand film | Full spine and every plan file; 06 is the cue sheet plus the timed stills board, a full animatic only with final music; review rounds by the crew row |
| Long or campaign: over 60 s, or chapters | narrated launch, UI walkthrough, brand film, music video, campaign | Full spine; a full animatic with the final audio before any expensive build; builders split by file; standard or full crew |
| Polish of an existing film | a film that "looks basic" | 01 writes a defect list against the type's QA thresholds; work opens the stage that owns each defect; change one element at a time, never restart |

**Supplied or partial material.** The router's intake table sets what each supplied item does to its stage. Differences on this route: a supplied concept still gets a structure option at 02 unless the structure was given too; a supplied script gets the first-3-seconds gate on its own opening (result reported, no new openings); a supplied storyboard is checked against the type's rhythm ranges and richness floor and its missing shot plan fields are filled; recorded voiceover or licensed music is the timing master at 06. Partial material: fill only the missing fields with this stage's method, log each fill in `ice/DIRECTION.md`, keep the user's own fields unchanged, and keep the stage `partial` until the fills are approved.

**Run files and changes.** Plan files in `ice/plan/`: `concepts.md`, `structure.md` (type, option, rejected options, length, aspect, fps, fingerprint line), `script.md` (the only home of the spoken words), `versions.md`, `hooks.md`, `storyboard.md`, `shot-plan.md`, `motion-plan.md`, `cue-sheet.json` (read by every builder, the sound pass and the captions). Every decision not yet in `ice/DIRECTION.md` goes there with its reason and source. Stages 01 to 03 loop until truth, structure and script agree. A later stage that exposes an earlier problem fixes the owning plan file, never the render. A change to a finished film edits the plan file, then the build, then renders a new versioned file, keeping the strong beats. The user's newest explicit note outranks earlier notes, brand rules and every default here.

## 4. Film types

Twelve types: product launch, app ad, brand film, logo sting, kinetic type, data story, social short, hype promo, UI walkthrough, music video, art film, industry spot. Read `references/film-setup.md` (section 1, Film types) at stage 01 to pick the type: the shared type file fields, the signals table, and what to do when two types fit.

## 5. Rules that are scoped, not global

Each source rule is right in its scope; the type file states which side applies. Timing master (voice-led, music-led, hybrid, free): the `ice-audio` skill, `references/cue-map-and-beat-sync.md` section 1, plus `references/cue-sheet.md` section 1. Cuts, crossfades, holds, camera during holds, end holds: the `ice-motion` skill, `SKILL.md` section 4.

## 6. Hard rules

- Approved numbers only; truth before style. Illustrative content is labelled.
- A real brand (named in the brief, or a reference brand for the field) appears only through its real material: live captures of its site and app, official brand or press kits, colours and fonts read from its own site, untouched official logos, UI rebuilt at 90 percent fidelity or better; never a guessed look. Third-party content inside its screens (cover art, user content, photos, real people's names) is replaced with licensed or neutral content (ice-assets).
- Full and alive: every shot meets the type's "Richness floor". "Premium" and "minimal" subtract clutter, never fullness (`ice-motion/references/composition-and-staging.md` section 7).
- Story, then frames, then style, then copy, then motion. A film styled before its story is a template.
- Three different options at every creative fork this workflow owns (structures, openings); a critic chooses, never the builder. Concept and direction counts follow the ice-direction tier.
- Carry, do not replace: every boundary has something that survives and moves, except inside a burst of hits and into the end card.
- The film differs from its nearest template or demo on at least 4 of 6 axes and from previous films in structure (`references/structure.md` section 7; ice-qa measures).
- Builders never grade their own work; reviewers are fresh and read-only.
- Stills before drafts, drafts before finals, finals as new files. Never overwrite a render.
- Never publish, post or schedule. Installs, downloads and spending happen only where the router's stops table (`ice-motion-director/SKILL.md` section 5, "Stops") allows; without a yes, take the path that needs none, log it, and say in the delivery note what a yes would add.

## 7. Crew size and sub-agent roles

Crews are Lean, Standard and Full, by film length and complexity. Read `references/film-setup.md` (section 2, Crew size and sub-agent roles) at stage 01 to pick the row, and at any stage when you need its critic, approver, review rounds or roles.

## 8. Noncommercial folder

`noncommercial/onetake/` holds PolyForm Noncommercial files. Open them only when `commercial_use` in `ice/state.json` is `no`, confirmed by the user in words. When it is `yes` or `unknown`, do not open, copy or adapt them and do not ask only to unlock them: this skill's references cover the same ground with permissive sources.
